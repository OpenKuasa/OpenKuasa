**Training** lists the courses and sessions for your team. Owners and admins add courses and keep track of who is enrolled. Employees can enrol themselves.

## Who can do this

- **Owners and admins** add, edit and remove courses, enrol people and mark them completed, outside the demo workspace.
- **Other members** linked to an employee record can enrol themselves, and withdraw before the course starts.

## Steps

![The Training screen with courses, enrolments and completion](/docs-media/lekiu/training.png)

### Add a course

1. Open **Lekiu**, then **Training** under **Performance**.
2. Click **Add course**.
3. Fill in the **Title**, and if you want the **Category (optional)** and **Provider (optional)**.
4. Choose the **First day** and the **Last day**.
5. Click **Add course**.

### Enrol people and mark completion

1. On the course's row, under **Manage**, click **People** (it shows how many are enrolled).
2. Choose an employee and click **Enrol**.
3. Once the course has started, click **Mark completed** for each person who finished it. **Undo completion** takes it back.
4. Click **Remove** next to a person to take them off the course.

Use **Edit** to change the course, or **Remove** and then **Remove course** to delete it.

### Enrol yourself

1. Find the course in **All training**.
2. Under **Your enrolment**, click **Enrol**. You can enrol until the course's last day.
3. To leave, click **Withdraw**. You can do this until the course starts.

## Common problems

- **"That course has already started. Ask an owner or admin to remove you."**
- **"That course has ended."**
- **"That course has not started yet."** Completion can be marked once the course has started.
- **"Someone is marked as completed on it. Undo that first."**
