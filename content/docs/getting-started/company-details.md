**Company details** holds your organisation's information: its legal name, numbers, address and logo.

## Who can do this

Workspace owners and admins can edit company details and the logo. Members and viewers can see the page, with a note saying **Only owners and admins can edit company details.** In the demo workspace the page is read-only.

## Steps

### Edit the details

1. Open the account menu (your name, top right) and click **Profile & Preferences**. In the side menu, under **Organisation**, choose **Company details**. Or go straight to [Company details](/account/company).
2. Under **Company**, fill in:
   - **Legal name** (required)
   - **Registration no**
   - **SST no**
   - **Tax identification number (TIN)**
   - **Website**
   - **Industry** and **Company size**
3. Under **Address**, fill in **Address line**, **City**, **State** and **Postcode**. **State** lists the Malaysian states and federal territories.
4. Click **Save changes**.

You see **Company details saved.** The new name shows across the app straight away.

![The Company card on the Company details page, with the legal name, registration, SST and tax number fields](/docs-media/getting-started/company-details.png)

### Add a logo

The logo is shown at the top of letters.

1. Under **Logo**, click **Upload logo** (or **Replace logo** if there is one already).
2. Choose a PNG, JPEG or WebP image, up to 1 MB.

You see **Logo saved.**

To take the logo off, click **Remove logo**. OpenKuasa asks **Remove the workspace logo? Letters will show the company name only.** Click **Remove logo** again to confirm, or **Keep** to leave it.

## Common problems

- **Please enter your company name.** **Legal name** cannot be empty.
- **A TIN is up to 20 letters and numbers.** Remove spaces, dashes and other marks from the TIN.
- **That file is not a PNG, JPEG or WebP image.** Choose an image in one of those types.
- **That image is over 1 MB.** Make the image smaller and try again.
- **That image could not be saved. Upload it again.** The upload did not finish. Try again.
- **Only owners and admins can edit company details.** Ask an owner or admin to make the change.
- **The demo workspace is read-only. Sign up to save changes.** The demo company cannot be changed.
