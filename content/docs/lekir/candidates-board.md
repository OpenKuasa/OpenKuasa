The **Pipeline board** on the Candidates screen shows every active application in one of five stages: Applied, Screening, Interview, Offer and Hired.

## Who can do this

Owners, admins and members. Viewers can see the board, but the cards have no actions menu.

## Steps

1. Open **Lekir** and choose **Candidates**.
2. Find the card on the **Pipeline board**. Each column shows the newest 50 in that stage.
3. Click the **…** button on the card to open its menu.
4. To change the stage, choose a **Move to …** item. You can move forward to any later stage, or back one stage with **Back to …**.
5. To rate the application, choose **Rate 1 out of 5** to **Rate 5 out of 5**. Choose **Clear rating** to remove it.
6. To end the application:
   - Choose **Reject** if you are turning the candidate down.
   - Choose **Mark withdrawn** if the candidate pulled out.
   - Pick a **Reason (optional)**: **Skills not a match**, **Not enough experience**, **Salary expectations**, **Role filled**, **Could not reach the candidate** or **Other**. Or leave it at **No reason**.
   - Click **Reject** or **Mark withdrawn** to confirm. The card leaves the board.
7. The menu also has **Schedule interview**, **Edit candidate**, **Delete application** and **Delete candidate**. It has **Add to talent pool** for someone not in the pool yet; for a pool member it shows **In the talent pool:** and their status.

## Common problems

- **An application can move back one stage at a time.** Move it back step by step.
- **This application is no longer active. Reopen it first.** A rejected or withdrawn application has to be reopened before it can move. Use **Reopen** in the [Applications](/docs/lekir/applications) details panel.
- **Interviews are not cancelled.** Rejecting or withdrawing an application does not cancel its scheduled interviews. Cancel them on the Interviews screen, and tell the candidate yourself.
- **The search box, filters and Export do nothing.** They are not ready yet (Coming soon).
