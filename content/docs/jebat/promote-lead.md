When a lead is worth following up in your CRM, promote it. Jebat makes a Kasturi contact from it and marks the lead **Promoted ✓**.

## Who can do this

Owners, admins and members can promote a lead. Viewers cannot. The demo workspace is read-only.

## Steps

1. Open **Jebat** and choose **Lead Funnel** in the side menu.
2. Find the lead in the **Leads** card.
3. Click **Promote** on its row.

The button changes to **Promoted ✓**. The new contact in Kasturi gets:

- the lead's name, split into first and last name;
- a status that follows the lead's stage: **Lead** becomes **New Leads**, **Contacted** and **Qualified** stay the same, **Booked** becomes **Qualified**, and **Won** becomes **Customer**;
- the lead's channel and source as tags.

Promoting does not delete or change the lead in Jebat.

## Common problems

- **"Already promoted to a contact."** This lead already has a Kasturi contact.
- **A lead shows Promote again after it was promoted.** Its contact was deleted in Kasturi. You can promote it again to make a new contact.
- **There is no Promote button, only "Open in Kasturi".** That lead came in through a lead form, a landing page or the chatbot, and is already a Kasturi contact.
