The demo lets you look around OpenKuasa before you sign up. It opens a shared sample workspace for a made-up company, Rimba Ventures.

## Who can do this

Anyone. You do not need an account or an email address.

## Steps

1. Go to the sign-in page. On the OpenKuasa website, click **Login**.
2. Click **Try the demo**. The button shows **Loading demo…** while the demo opens.
3. You land on Tuah, the home screen. Open any product from the navigation to look around.

## What the demo can and cannot do

- You join the demo as a viewer. You can look at every screen, but the demo is read-only: nothing you change is saved.
- The assistants give sample answers in the demo. They do not use AI, and nothing you ask is saved.
- Screens that save things, such as your profile, password, two-step sign-in and team, show a note saying they are read-only in the demo.
- To use OpenKuasa with your own data, sign up for your own workspace. See [Create your account and workspace](/docs/getting-started/create-your-workspace).

## Common problems

- **Demo is unavailable right now.** The demo could not open. Try again later.
- **You are browsing as a demo guest. Sign out of the demo, then sign in or create an account as … to join.** You opened an invite link while still in the demo. Click **Sign out**, then open the invite again.
- **The demo is read-only. Sign up with your email to create your own workspace.** You tried to create a workspace while signed in to the demo. Sign out first, then sign up.
