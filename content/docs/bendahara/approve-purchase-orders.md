A purchase order that has been submitted waits as **Awaiting approval** until an owner or admin approves or rejects it.

## Who can do this

- Only owners and admins can approve, reject or close a purchase order.
- An admin cannot approve an order they raised or submitted themselves. Someone else has to approve it. An owner can approve their own.
- Members and viewers cannot approve. In the demo workspace nothing can be changed.

## Steps

### Approve or reject an order

1. Open **Purchase Orders** under **Purchases** in the Bendahara menu. The **Awaiting approval** card shows how many are waiting.
2. Find the order. You can choose **Awaiting approval** in the status list.
3. Open the row's menu and choose **Approve** or **Reject**. The card shows the order's lines and total.
4. To approve, click **Approve**. Once approved, deliveries can be recorded and bills raised against it.
5. To reject, type the **Reason** and click **Reject**. The order goes back to draft.

### Close an approved order

1. Open the row's menu on an approved order and choose **Close order**.
2. Confirm with **Close order**. Use this once the order is fully dealt with. A closed order cannot be changed or reopened.

## Common problems

- **"You raised or submitted this purchase order, so someone else has to approve it."** Ask another owner or admin.
- **"Say why the order is rejected."** A rejection needs a reason.
- **"Only an owner or admin can do that to a purchase order."** You are a member.
