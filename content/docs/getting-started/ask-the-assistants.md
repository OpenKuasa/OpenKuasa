OpenKuasa has an AI assistant for your whole business, and one for each product. You ask them in plain words.

## Who can do this

Every signed-in person, whatever their role. In the demo workspace the assistants give sample answers instead of real ones.

## Where to ask

- **Tuah**, the home screen, is where you talk to Sari (Taming Sari): one assistant for your whole business.
- **Ask Sari**, the button at the bottom of product screens, opens the same assistant beside the screen you are on.
- Each product's **Overview** screen has its own assistant card:
  - **Jebat**, your CMO: win new leads with AI ads
  - **Kasturi**, your sales co-pilot: manage and close your pipeline
  - **Lekiu**, your HR co-pilot: build and manage your team
  - **Lekir**, your hiring lead: recruit and hire your next team
  - **Bendahara**, your CFO: accounting and SST

## Steps

1. Open Tuah, click **Ask Sari**, or open a product's **Overview** screen.
2. Type your question in the box.
3. Press Enter, or click the send button next to the box.

## Free questions

Until your workspace adds its own OpenRouter key, each person gets 3 free questions a week. They are shared across all the assistants. They reset every Monday at 00:00 UTC.

Each chat shows how many are left, for example **2 of 3 free questions left this week.**

A free question also has limits:

- The assistant only sees the last few messages of the chat.
- It can carry one small file, up to 2 MB.

When the free questions are used up, chat stops until next week, or until an owner or admin adds a key. See [Add your OpenRouter key](/docs/getting-started/openrouter-key).

## Common problems

- **You have used your 3 free questions this week.** Wait until next week, or ask an owner or admin to add an OpenRouter key.
- **Ask a workspace owner or admin to add an OpenRouter key to keep chatting.** Members and viewers cannot add the key themselves.
- **A free question can carry one small file (up to 2 MB). Add your OpenRouter key to attach more.** Send a smaller file, or one file at a time.
- **Sign up free to chat with …** You are in the demo. Sign up to get real answers.
