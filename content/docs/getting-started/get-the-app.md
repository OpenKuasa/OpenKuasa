You can install OpenKuasa from the website. It opens in its own window with its own icon. You do not need an app store or a download.

## Where to find it

Look for **Get the app**. It is in the header and footer of the OpenKuasa website, and in your account menu once you are signed in. It is hidden inside the installed app, because you already have it.

## If your browser shows an install prompt

Click **Get the app** and follow the prompt from your browser.

## If you see steps instead

Some browsers do not offer a prompt. Then **Get the app** shows the steps for your browser.

### Chrome on a computer

1. Click the three dots at the top right of Chrome.
2. Choose **Cast, save, and share**, then **Install OpenKuasa**.

### Edge on a computer

1. Click the three dots at the top right of Edge.
2. Choose **Apps**, then **Install this site as an app**.

### Chrome on Android

1. Tap the three dots at the top right of Chrome.
2. Tap **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.

### Safari on iPhone or iPad

1. Tap Share in Safari.
2. Choose **Add to Home Screen**.

### Safari on a Mac

1. In the menu bar, choose **File**.
2. Choose **Add to Dock**.

## Other browsers

Some browsers, such as Firefox, cannot install apps. Open openkuasa.com in Chrome, Edge or Safari instead.
