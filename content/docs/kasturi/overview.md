The Overview is Kasturi's home screen. It shows your sales figures, worked out from your own deals, contacts and follow-ups, and a chat box to ask Kasturi about them.

## Who can do this

Everyone in the workspace can open the Overview. The figures come from the workspace's own data, so everyone sees the same numbers.

## Steps

1. Open **Kasturi** and choose **Overview**.
2. Read the four figures at the top:
   - **Pipeline value**: the value of every open deal, in ringgit.
   - **Open deals**: how many deals are still open.
   - **Win rate**: deals won out of deals closed (won and lost).
   - **Revenue won · this month**: the value of deals won this calendar month.
3. Look at the charts below them:
   - **Deals over time** shows deals created and won in each of the last 8 weeks.
   - **Deals by stage** and **Pipeline value** show your open deals in each stage of your default pipeline.
   - **Deals by owner** shows how many open deals each person has.
   - **Win rate** shows the share of closed deals that were won, as a dial.
4. Check **Deals closing soon**. It lists up to five open deals whose expected close date is in the next 7 days.
5. Check **Recent activity**. It shows the latest deals added, won or lost, contacts added, logged calls and messages, and follow-ups done.
6. Check **Follow-ups**. It lists open follow-ups, soonest first. An overdue one shows **Overdue** in red.
7. To ask a question, type it in the Kasturi chat box at the top, or pick one of the ready questions, such as **How much is my pipeline worth?**

## Common problems

- **Couldn't load your dashboard — please refresh**: the figures could not be read. Reload the page.
- **No deals yet**: the workspace has no deals. Add one on the Deals screen.
- **No deal has closed yet**: the win rate needs at least one deal marked won or lost.
- A deal moving from one stage to another is not shown in **Recent activity**. Only adding, winning and losing a deal are.
- In the demo workspace, the chat gives a sample answer. Sign up to ask about your own data.
- Each person has 3 free questions a week across all the assistants, unless your workspace adds its own OpenRouter key.
