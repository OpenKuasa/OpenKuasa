Financial Reports works your statements out of every entry in the books. Pick a period and every report follows it.

## Who can do this

Everyone in the workspace can see the reports and export them.

## Steps

![The Financial Reports screen with revenue, expenses, net profit and the Profit and Loss report](/docs-media/bendahara/financial-reports.png)

1. Open **Reports** under **Analytics** in the Bendahara menu.
2. Choose the **Period**: **This month**, **Last month**, **This quarter**, **This year**, **Last year**, **All time** or **Custom**. For **Custom**, pick the **From** and **To** months.
3. The top cards show **Revenue**, **Expenses**, **Net profit** and **Profit margin** for the period.
4. Read the reports below:
   - **Profit & Loss**: income and expenses for the period.
   - **Balance Sheet**: assets, liabilities and equity, as at the end of the period, with current earnings.
   - **Trial Balance**: the debits and credits of each account in the period.
   - **Cash Flow**: opening cash, what came in and went out, and closing cash.
   - **Aged Receivables** and **Aged Payables**: what customers owe you and what you owe suppliers, by how long it has been due (**Not yet due**, **1–30 days**, **31–60 days**, **61–90 days** and **Over 90 days**), as at today.
5. Click **Export CSV** on a report to download it.

To see every entry behind the figures, click **Open the general ledger**. See [Journals](/docs/bendahara/journals).
