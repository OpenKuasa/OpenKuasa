After each run, Weekly Studio may suggest changes to your Jebat data. Each suggestion waits under **Pending approvals** until someone approves or rejects it.

## Who can do this

Owners, admins and members can approve or reject a suggestion. Viewers see the suggestions with the note **Ask an owner to review this.** The demo workspace cannot approve anything.

## What it can suggest

Weekly Studio picks its suggestions from these checks on your data:

- A lead at **Won** that is not a Kasturi contact yet: promote it to a contact.
- A lead that has had a completed appointment but has not reached **Booked**: move it to **Booked**.
- A lead at **Qualified** or **Booked** with no upcoming appointment: book a follow-up call three days from now.
- When the run found market ideas for the week, and no form was suggested in the last 30 days: create a lead form as a draft, with a name it picks. The form stays a draft until you activate it.

## Steps

1. Open **Jebat** and choose **AI Agents** in the side menu.
2. Under **Pending approvals**, read each suggestion. It shows the change, a short reason for it, and every value the change will set.
3. Click **Approve** to make the change, or **Reject** to dismiss it. Nothing is changed when you reject.
4. To see what was decided lately, open **Recently decided**. Each item shows **Approved**, **Rejected**, **Failed** or **Expired**.

## Common problems

- **"No suggestions waiting — Jebat will add some after its next run."** There is nothing to decide. Run Weekly Studio again later; see [Turn on and run Weekly Studio](/docs/jebat/weekly-studio).
- **"You do not have permission to make changes here."** Viewers cannot decide on suggestions. Ask an owner, admin or member.
- **A suggestion shows Failed.** The change could not be made. You can make it yourself on the screen it is about.
