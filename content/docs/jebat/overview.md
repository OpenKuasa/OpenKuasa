The Jebat overview puts your leads, campaigns and appointments on one screen, with Jebat's chat box at the top.

## Who can do this

Everyone in the workspace can open the overview: owners, admins, members and viewers.

## Steps

1. Open **Jebat** and choose **Overview** in the side menu.
2. To ask Jebat a question, type it into **Ask Jebat anything…** at the top, or click a ready question such as **Which ad is performing best?**
3. Read the four figures at the top:
   - **Total leads**: every lead in Jebat, including leads that came in through your lead forms.
   - **Ad spend**: the spend you entered on your campaigns in [Ad Studio](/docs/jebat/ad-studio).
   - **Cost / lead**: your total ad spend divided by the leads your campaigns brought in.
   - **Conversion**: the share of your leads that reached **Won**.
4. Look over the cards below:
   - **Leads over time**: new leads each week for the last 8 weeks, and how many of them reached **Qualified** or further.
   - **Leads by channel**: where your leads came from.
   - **Spend by channel**: your campaign spend, split by channel.
   - **Lead funnel**: how many leads reached each stage, from **Leads** to **Won**.
   - **Top campaigns**: your campaigns, lowest cost per lead first.
   - **AI agents**: how many suggestions from Weekly Studio are waiting for you. Click **Open AI Agents →** to review them.
   - **Upcoming appointments**: your next five scheduled appointments, soonest first.

## Common problems

- **"Ad engine health" and "Best time to engage" say "Not available yet".** These cards are not built for your own workspace yet. Only the demo shows sample figures in them.
- **A card says "No leads yet" or "No campaigns yet".** Add a lead in [Lead Funnel](/docs/jebat/lead-funnel) or a campaign in [Ad Studio](/docs/jebat/ad-studio), and the cards fill in.
- **"Couldn't load your dashboard — please refresh".** Jebat could not read your data. Refresh the page.
- **Jebat stops answering.** Without your own AI key, each person gets a few free questions a week. See [Add your OpenRouter key](/docs/getting-started/openrouter-key).
