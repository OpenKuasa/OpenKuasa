The **Shift Calendar** shows one week's shifts, Monday to Sunday. Owners and admins plan the roster here.

## Who can do this

- **Owners and admins** edit the roster and the shift types, outside the demo workspace.
- **Managers** see their own shifts and those of the people who report to them.
- **Everyone else** sees their own shifts.

## Steps

![The Shift Calendar screen with the weekly shift grid and shift types](/docs-media/lekiu/shift-calendar.png)

_Shown from the sample workspace, where editing is switched off._

### Set the week's shifts

1. Open **Lekiu**, then **Shift Calendar** under **Attendance**.
2. Use the arrows or **This week** to pick the week.
3. In the **Shift grid**, pick a shift in each cell, or **—** for none. Changed cells are marked until you save.
4. To fill the empty cells from the week before, click **Copy last week**.
5. Click **Save week**. To throw your changes away instead, click **Discard**.

Inactive employees are shown but cannot be changed.

### Manage shift types

1. Under the grid, click **Shift types**.
2. Click **Add a shift type**, or **Edit** next to an existing one.
3. Fill in the **Name** (up to 40 characters) and pick a **Colour**.
4. Tick **An off day (no times)**, or set **Starts** and **Ends**. A shift that ends at or before its start runs overnight. Leave both times empty for a shift with no set hours.
5. Click **Add type** or **Save type**.

Click **Archive** to stop a type being picked. Past shifts keep it. Click **Bring back** to use it again.

## Common problems

- **"One of those shift types is archived, so it cannot be picked. Choose another type."**
- **"One of those employees is inactive, so their shifts cannot be changed."**
- **"There is already a shift type with that name."**
- **"Nothing to copy: last week has no shifts for the empty cells."**
