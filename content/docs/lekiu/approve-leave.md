**Leave Approvals** lists the leave requests from your team, newest first, with the ones waiting for a decision marked.

## Who can do this

- **Owners and admins** see every request. They can approve, reject and cancel, and add leave for any active employee.
- **Managers** (people others report to) see only the requests of the people who report to them. They can approve and reject pending requests. They cannot cancel or add leave.
- Everyone else sees "This page is for owners and admins of the workspace."

An admin cannot approve or reject their own leave. Their row shows **Needs the owner or another admin**. The workspace owner can decide their own.

## Steps

![The Leave Approvals screen with pending requests and Approve, Reject and Cancel buttons](/docs-media/lekiu/approve-leave.png)

### Approve or reject

1. Open **Lekiu**, then **Leave** under **Approvals**.
2. Find the request in **Leave requests**. A pending request has a live dot and the **Pending** status. If it asks for more days than the balance, it shows **Over balance by** and the number of days.
3. Click **Approve**. Or click **Reject**, type a **Note (optional)** and click **Confirm reject**. The employee sees the note on their **Leave** screen.

### Add leave for an employee

1. Click **Add leave for…**.
2. Choose the **Employee**.
3. Fill in the leave form as in [Apply for leave](/docs/lekiu/apply-for-leave), then click **Apply for leave**. The leave goes into the queue as pending.

### Cancel leave

1. On the request's row, click **Cancel**, then **Confirm**. For approved leave, the days go back to the person's balance. This cannot be undone.

## Common problems

- **"You can't approve, reject or pay your own request. Ask the workspace owner or another admin."** You are an admin deciding your own request.
- **"That request has already been decided or cancelled."** Someone else got there first. The page reloads to show where it stands.
- **"Only owners, admins and the employee's manager can do that."** You are not an approver for this request.
- **Export does nothing**: **Export** is not built yet.
