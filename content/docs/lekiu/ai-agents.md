Lekiu's **AI Agents** screen runs an agent that watches your team records for what you ask, and suggests notes for HR to approve. Nothing is saved until you approve it.

## Who can do this

Owners and admins. Everyone else sees "This page is for owners and admins of the workspace." The demo workspace shows a sample.

## Before you start

The agent runs on your workspace's own AI key. See [Add your OpenRouter key](/docs/getting-started/openrouter-key).

## Steps

1. Open **Lekiu**, then **AI Agents** under **Configuration**.
2. In **Focus**, tell the agent what to watch for in your team records, in your own words.
3. In **Schedule and limits**, choose when it runs and what it may spend.
4. When it has suggestions, they appear under **Waiting for you**. Click **Approve** on a suggestion, or **Dismiss**.
5. Approved notes are listed under **Notes from the agent**. **Recent history** shows each run, how many suggestions it made and what it cost.

How focus, schedules, limits and approvals work is the same across OpenKuasa. See [AI agents and focus](/docs/tuah/ai-agents).
