Each lead form keeps what visitors sent through it. You can read the latest submissions on the Lead Forms screen.

## Who can do this

- Everyone in the workspace can read submissions.
- Owners, admins and members can delete one. Viewers cannot.
- The demo workspace's forms are samples, so they have no submissions to show.

## Steps

1. Open **Jebat** and choose **Lead Forms** in the side menu.
2. In the form's row, click **View** beside its **Contacts** count.
3. The **Submissions** card opens above the table, newest first. Each submission shows the name, **Email**, **Phone**, **Message** and **Consent**: when the visitor agreed, and the exact sentence they agreed to.
4. To open the contact the submission made, click **Contact in Kasturi**.
5. To delete a submission, click **Delete**, then **Delete submission**. The contact it made stays in Kasturi. This cannot be undone.
6. Click **Close** when you are done.

## Common problems

- **"No submissions to show for this form yet."** Nobody has sent this form yet. Check that it is **Active** and that you have shared its link; see [Create and share a lead form](/docs/jebat/lead-forms).
- **"The submissions could not be loaded. Please try again."** Click **Try again**.
- **"Its contact has been deleted."** The contact this submission made was deleted in Kasturi. The submission is still kept.
- **Consent says "Not recorded (sent before consent was asked for)".** The form was sent before forms asked for consent.
