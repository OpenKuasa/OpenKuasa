The talent pool holds people you want to keep in touch with for future roles. Each person in it has a status.

- **Available**: Open to hearing about a role now.
- **Passive**: Not looking right now. Worth keeping in touch for later.
- **Re-engaged**: You have been back in touch and put them forward for a job again.

The screen also shows **Shortlisted** for someone with a live application at Interview or beyond.

## Who can do this

- **Search the pool:** everyone in the workspace, viewers included.
- **Add, change, move, remove or delete:** owners, admins and members.

## Steps

### Add people to the pool

1. Open **Lekir** and choose **Talent Pool**.
2. Click **Add to Pool**. The list shows people on file who are not in the pool yet.
3. Choose a status under **Add as**.
4. Type in **Search by name or headline** to find someone, then click **Add** next to them.
5. Click **Done** when you are finished.

You can also click **Add to talent pool** in an application's details panel, or on a card's menu on the Candidates board. That adds them as Available.

### Change a status or move someone to a job

1. In **Pool candidates**, find the person. Use **Search by name, skill or location…** if the list is long.
2. To change their status, click the **…** button and pick a status under **Pool status**.
3. To put them forward for a job, click **Move to pipeline**, choose the **Job** and click **Move to pipeline**. They get a new application at Applied and are marked Re-engaged. They stay in the talent pool.
4. To take them out of the pool, click **…**, then **Remove from pool**, and confirm. They stay on file with their details and applications.
5. To delete them for good, click **…**, then **Delete candidate**, and confirm. This can't be undone.

## Common problems

- **There are no open or paused jobs. Open a job first.** Moving to the pipeline needs an open or paused job.
- **… is already in the talent pool.** Nothing was changed.
- **Everyone on file is already in the talent pool.** There is no one left to add.
- **Only the newest 1,000 pool members are read.** Someone put on file long ago may be missing from the list and from search.
- **The role menu and filters do nothing.** **All roles**, **Filter** and **All sources** are not ready yet (Coming soon).
