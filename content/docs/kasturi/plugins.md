Plugins shows what Kasturi is connected to and where each connection is set up. It also lists what people often ask for that is not built yet.

## Who can do this

Everyone in the workspace can open Plugins. Each connection is set up on the screen it links to, under that screen's own rules.

## Steps

1. Open **Kasturi** and choose **Plugins**.
2. Under **Connected services**, check each row:
   - **AI key (OpenRouter)**: **Set** or **Not set**. It links to the AI key page in your account.
   - **Email sending (Resend)**: the address broadcasts are sent from, or **Not set**. It links to Broadcast.
   - **Chatbot on landing pages**: how many published pages show the chatbot, or **Off**. It links to AI Chatbot.
   - **Automations**: how many automations are switched on, or **None on**. It links to Automations.
3. Click the link on a row (**Manage**, **Add a key**, **Set up** or **Open**) to change that connection.

![The Plugins screen with connected services](/docs-media/kasturi/plugins.png)

### Not available yet

These are listed so you do not look for a switch that is not there. There is nothing to switch on for them today:

- WhatsApp, for the chatbot and for broadcasts.
- Instagram and Messenger, for the chatbot.
- Online payments, to collect on invoices.
- Calendar sync, to sync appointments both ways.
- Connecting other apps.
- LHDN MyInvois. Bendahara keeps the e-invoice details on each invoice, but nothing is sent to LHDN yet.

## Common problems

- A row shows **Unknown** when its count could not be read. Reload the page.
- **Couldn't load your connections — please refresh**: reload the page.
