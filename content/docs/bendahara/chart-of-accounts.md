Chart of Accounts is your double-entry account structure. It lists each account by kind (asset, liability, equity, income and expense) with its balance. A card says whether the books balance: **Books balance**, or how far they are out.

## Who can do this

- Everyone in the workspace can see the chart and export it.
- Only owners and admins can add, edit, archive, unarchive or delete accounts. Only they see **New account** and the row menus.
- In the demo workspace nothing can be changed.

## Steps

![The Chart of Accounts grouped by asset and liability, with the Books balance card](/docs-media/bendahara/chart-of-accounts.png)

### Add an account

1. Open **Chart of Accounts** under **Analytics** in the Bendahara menu.
2. Click **New account**.
3. Enter a **Code** of 1 to 10 characters and a **Name**.
4. Choose the **Type**. It cannot be changed afterwards.
5. Click **Add account**.

The **Stands for** column says what an account is: **System**, **Bank or cash account**, **Category** or **Added by you**. Bank and cash accounts come from [Banking](/docs/bendahara/banking), and category accounts from [Expense and income categories](/docs/bendahara/categories).

### Edit, archive or delete an account

1. Open the account's row menu.
2. Choose **Edit**, **Archive** or **Unarchive**.
3. Only an account you added can be deleted, and only while it has no journal lines. Confirm with **Delete account**.

Tick **Show archived** to see archived accounts. Click **Export** to download the list.

## Common problems

- **"That code or name is already used by another account."**
- **"This account has journal lines, so it cannot be deleted. Archive it instead."**
- **"That cannot be done to this account: it stands for a bank account, a category or a system account, or it is in use."**
- **"Only an owner or admin can change accounts or journals."**
