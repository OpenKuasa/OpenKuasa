Departments group your employees. They are shown in the directory, on charts and in filters.

## Who can do this

Owners and admins, outside the demo workspace. Everyone else sees the list of departments and how many people are in each.

## Steps

1. Open **Lekiu**, then **Employees** under **Management**.
2. Find the **Departments** card.
3. To add one, type the name in **New department** (up to 80 characters) and click **Add**.
4. To rename one, click the pencil next to it, type the new name and click **Save**.
5. To delete one, click the bin next to it, then **Delete?** to confirm.

To put an employee in a department, edit the employee and choose the **Department**.

## Common problems

- **"That department still has employees, including inactive ones. Move them to another department first."** Edit each of those employees and pick another department, then delete it.
- **"Give the department a name."** The name cannot be empty.
