**Payment Vouchers** is the register of vouchers on record, newest first. It only shows information. Creating vouchers is not built yet.

## Who can do this

Owners and admins. The screen is hidden from everyone else, and shows "This page is for owners and admins of the workspace." if opened.

## Steps

1. Open **Lekiu**, then **Payment Vouchers** under **Payroll**.
2. Read the figures: **Issued this month**, **Paid this month**, **Drafts** and **Outstanding** (issued but not paid).
3. Read **Amount by month** and **Vouchers by type**.
4. In **Vouchers**, each row shows the **Voucher no**, **Payee**, **Type**, **Amount**, **Date** and **Status**: **Paid**, **Issued** or **Draft**.

There is no way to create vouchers yet: **New voucher** and **Export** show "Available in a later update". In your own workspace the register shows "No vouchers yet" until vouchers can be created.
