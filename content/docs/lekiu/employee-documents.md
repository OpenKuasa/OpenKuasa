Owners and admins keep each employee's documents on their record, from the **Employees** screen. The employee sees them on **My Documents**.

## Who can do this

Owners and admins, outside the demo workspace. The **Documents** button is only shown to them.

## Before you start

Have the file ready: a PDF or an image (JPEG, PNG, WebP or HEIC), up to 10 MB.

## Steps

### Add a document

1. Open **Lekiu**, then **Employees** under **Management**.
2. On the employee's row, click **Documents**.
3. Click **Add document for** and the employee's name.
4. Fill in the **Title** and the **Type**: **Contract**, **Tax form**, **Benefits**, **Identity**, **Medical**, **Certificate** or **Other**. Add an expiry date under **Expires (optional)** if it has one.
5. Click **Choose file** and pick the file.
6. Click **Add document**.

### Ask the employee to acknowledge it

1. On the document, click **Request acknowledgement**.
2. The employee sees **I have read this** on **My Documents**. This records that they have read it; it is not a signature.

To stop asking, click **Withdraw request**.

### Change, replace or remove a document

- **Edit** changes the title, type or expiry date.
- **Replace file** swaps the file.
- **Remove**, then **Remove document**, deletes the document and its file. This cannot be undone.

A document past its expiry date shows **Expired**. One close to it shows **Expiring soon**.

## Common problems

- **"Choose a file to upload."** A new document needs a file.
- **"That file is larger than 10 MB."** Use a smaller file.
- **"Use a PDF or an image (JPEG, PNG, WebP or HEIC)."** The file is another type.
- **"That is 200 documents already. Remove one first."**
