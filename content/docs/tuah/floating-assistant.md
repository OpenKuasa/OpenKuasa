The **Ask Sari** button sits at the bottom right of every product screen. It opens the same assistant as the Tuah page, in a panel beside your work.

## Who can do this

Anyone signed in. It works the same as asking on the Tuah page, with the same free questions and the same rules for changes.

The button is not on the Tuah page itself, because that page is the assistant at full size.

## Steps

1. On any product screen, click **Ask Sari** at the bottom right.
2. Type your question and press Enter.

![The Ask Sari panel open beside a Kasturi screen in the demo workspace](/docs-media/tuah/floating-assistant.png)

The panel knows which screen you opened it from. Under its title it says, for example, **Asking from Kasturi › Contacts**. That screen is sent with your question, so "how do I add one here?" gets steps for that screen.

The suggested questions match the product you are in. In the demo, the panel shows general ones.

At the top of the panel:

- the panel icon at the left (**Open your chats**) shows your saved chats;
- **New chat** starts a fresh chat;
- **Open in Tuah** opens the same chat on the full Tuah page;
- **Close** closes the panel. Pressing Escape, or clicking outside the panel, also closes it.

In your own workspace, the panel remembers its chat while you move between screens in the same browser tab. Close it and open it again, and your chat is still there.

## Common problems

- **The panel says the free questions are used up.** It shares the weekly free questions with every other assistant. See [Add your OpenRouter key](/docs/getting-started/openrouter-key).
- **The button covers the last row of a page.** Scroll down. Each screen leaves room at the bottom so the last content can scroll clear of the button.
