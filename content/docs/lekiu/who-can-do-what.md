What you can do in Lekiu depends on three things: your role in the workspace, whether people report to you, and whether your account is linked to your employee record.

## Who can do this

This guide explains the rules. It does not change anything.

## Owners and admins (HR)

Owners and admins can do all of Lekiu's HR work:

- add, edit, deactivate and delete employees, and link accounts to employee records;
- approve, reject and cancel leave, time-off, claims and overtime for anyone, and mark claims as paid;
- correct attendance, plan the roster, keep the public holiday list and change the working week;
- write letters, announcements, reviews and training courses;
- open **Payroll**, **Payment Vouchers**, **Settings** and **AI Agents**. These screens are hidden from everyone else.

An admin cannot approve, reject or mark as paid their own request. The row shows **Needs the owner or another admin**. The workspace owner can decide their own requests.

## Managers

You are a manager in Lekiu when other employees have you in their **Reports to** field, and your account is linked to your own employee record. Only owners and admins can set **Reports to**.

A manager sees the approval screens and **Attendance** under **Management**. On them, a manager can:

- approve or reject the pending requests of the people who report to them directly;
- approve or reject a shift swap between two of their reports;
- read their reports' attendance, timesheets and shifts.

A manager cannot cancel someone else's request, add a request for someone, mark a claim as paid, or correct attendance.

## Everyone with a linked employee record

Any member of the workspace whose account is linked to an employee record can, for themselves:

- apply for leave and time-off, make expense claims and claim overtime, and cancel them while the rules allow;
- clock in and out, and record their own hours for this week and last week;
- ask to swap a shift with a colleague, and accept or decline a swap asked of them;
- see their own records, documents, letters, goals, reviews and training, upload some documents and acknowledge letters.

Applying for and cancelling your own leave, time-off, claims and overtime works for the **Viewer** role too.

## If your account is not linked

You can still see the team directory, but your own leave, claims and payslips are not shown. Screens about you show "Your HR record isn't linked yet". Ask an owner or admin to link your account. See [Link an account to an employee record](/docs/lekiu/link-an-account).

## In the demo workspace

In the demo you can open every screen, but nothing can be saved. Buttons that would change something are switched off, and some say "Available in a later update" or "Sign up to try this with your own team."
