**My Goals** lists what you are working towards, with the progress of each. You keep your own goals here.

## Who can do this

- **Anyone linked to an employee record** can add, update and remove their own goals, outside the demo workspace.
- **Owners and admins** can also keep an employee's goals: on the **Employees** screen, click **Goals** on their row, then **Add goal for** and their name.

## Before you start

Your account must be linked to your employee record.

## Steps

### Add a goal

1. Open **Lekiu**, then **My Goals** under **General**.
2. Click **Add Goal**.
3. Fill in the **Title**, the **Progress (%)** from 0 to 100, and a **Due date (optional)**.
4. Under **Where it stands**, choose **On track** or **At risk**. This is not asked when progress is 100%.
5. Click **Add goal**.

### Update progress

1. On the goal's card, change **Progress (%)**, and **Where it stands** if needed.
2. Click **Update**.

A goal at 100% shows **Done**.

### Edit or remove a goal

On the card, click **Edit** to change the title or due date, or **Remove**, then **Remove goal**.

## Common problems

- **"That is 30 open goals already. Finish or remove one first."**
- **"Progress must be a whole number from 0 to 100."**
