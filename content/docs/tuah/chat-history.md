Your chats with Tuah are saved, so you can pick one up later. Chats from the **Ask Sari** panel on other screens are saved to the same list.

## Who can do this

Anyone signed in to a workspace. The list shows only your own chats in the workspace you are in. It says **Only you can see your chats**.

The demo does not save chats. Its list says **Chats are not saved in the demo**.

## Steps

### Open the list

On a wide screen, the list is the column at the left of the Tuah page. On a narrow screen, tap the panel icon at the top left (**Open your chats**). In the **Ask Sari** panel, it is the same icon at the top left.

Chats are grouped by when you last asked something: **Today**, **Yesterday**, **Previous 7 days**, **Previous 30 days** and **Older**. A chat is named after its first question.

### Find a chat

1. Type in **Search chats**. It matches chat names.
2. Click a chat to open it.

The open chat's address ends in `?chat=` and its id. A refresh, or the back button, brings you back to it.

### Start a new chat

Click **New chat**.

### Rename a chat

1. Point at the chat and click the **…** button beside it.
2. Choose **Rename**.
3. Type the new name, up to 120 characters, and press Enter. Press Escape to cancel.

### Delete a chat

1. Click the **…** button beside the chat.
2. Choose **Delete**.
3. The row asks **Delete this chat?**. Click **Delete** to confirm, or **Cancel**.

Deleting a chat removes its messages and any files sent in it. It cannot be brought back.

## Common problems

- **A chat about HR is not in the list.** When Tuah looks at HR data (staff, leave, claims, attendance, payroll and the like) to answer, that question and its answer are not saved. This keeps HR details out of saved chats.
- **"That chat is no longer available, so here is a new one."** The chat was deleted, or it belongs to another workspace.
- **"This chat could not be opened".** It is still saved. Check your connection and click **Try again**.
- **"Your chats could not be loaded".** Nothing is lost. Click **Try again**.
- **"Could not rename. Try again."** or **"Could not delete. Try again."** The change did not go through. Try once more.
