Lekir's Settings screen brings your recruiting preferences together. You change the application form here. The other cards show where things stand and link to the screen where they are changed.

## Who can do this

- **Open Settings:** everyone in the workspace.
- **Change the Application form:** owners, admins and members.

## Steps

1. Open **Lekir** and choose **Settings** under **Configuration**.
2. **Workspace** shows your company and team, set in your account and used across every product. Use its links to change them.
3. **Application form** has four switches for what applicants must or can give. Change them and click **Save**. See [The online application form](/docs/lekir/application-form).
4. **Hiring pipeline** lists the five stages, Applied, Screening, Interview, Offer and Hired, with how many live applications sit in each now. The stages are the same in every workspace. Click **Open Applications** to work on them.
5. **Careers page** shows whether your public page is **On** or **Off**, and how many jobs are showing on it. Click **Manage careers page** to change it, or **View the public page** while it is on.

## Common problems

- **"N open jobs have no description, so the page leaves them out."** The Careers page card shows this with the number of jobs (it says "would leave" while the page is off). Edit each job and add a description.
- **You do not have permission to change this.** Viewers cannot change the application form.
- **Not available in the demo.** The demo's settings cannot be changed.
- **Couldn't load your hiring data — please refresh.** A card could not be read. Refresh the page.
