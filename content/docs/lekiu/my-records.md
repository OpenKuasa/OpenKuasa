**My Records** shows your own employee record: what HR has on file about you. You can read it here, but not change it.

## Who can do this

Anyone in the workspace whose account is linked to their employee record, whatever their role. You only ever see your own record.

## Before you start

Your account must be linked to your employee record.

## Steps

1. Open **Lekiu**, then **Records** under **General**.
2. Read the top row: **Tenure**, **Annual leave left**, **Department** and **Employment**.
3. Read the cards:
   - **Personal**: name, NRIC, date of birth, email and phone.
   - **Employment**: employee number, department, designation, join date, type and status.
   - **Statutory**: your EPF / KWSP, SOCSO / PERKESO and income tax numbers.
   - **Emergency Contact** and **Bank Details**. Only the last four characters of your bank account are shown.

Anything HR has not filled in shows **Not recorded**.

## Common problems

- **A detail is wrong or missing**: only an owner or admin can change it, on the **Employees** screen. Ask them.
- **"Your HR record isn't linked yet"**: ask an owner or admin to link your account. See [Link an account to an employee record](/docs/lekiu/link-an-account).
