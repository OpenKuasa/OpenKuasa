On the **OT Claims** screen you claim the overtime you worked. The pay is worked out for you from the kind of day and your salary.

## Who can do this

Anyone in the workspace whose account is linked to their employee record, whatever their role. You can only claim your own overtime here. Owners and admins enter overtime for someone else on the overtime approvals screen.

## Before you start

Your account must be linked to your employee record.

## Steps

![The OT Claims screen with overtime by month, by rate and the My requests table](/docs-media/lekiu/ot-claims.png)

_Shown from the sample workspace, where editing is switched off._

1. Open **Lekiu**, then **OT Claims** under **Applications**.
2. Click **Claim OT**.
3. Choose the **Date worked**. It cannot be in the future.
4. Type the **Hours**, in whole or half hours, such as 2 or 2.5.
5. Add a **Reason (optional)** if you want, up to 500 characters.
6. Check the line under the form. It shows the kind of day (**Weekday**, **Rest day** or **Public holiday**), the rate it is paid at, and the amount.
7. Click **Submit overtime**. The claim appears under **My requests** as **Pending**.

If no salary is on file for you yet, the amount shows **Not calculated**. It is worked out once HR sets your salary.

### Cancel an overtime claim

You can cancel only while the claim is pending and it has not been paid in a payroll run. Click **Cancel** on its row, then **Confirm**. Cancelling cannot be undone.

## Common problems

- **"Overtime is at least half an hour."** Enter 0.5 hours or more.
- **"Overtime cannot be more than 24 hours in a day."** Check the hours.
- **"Use whole or half hours."** Round to the nearest half hour.
- **"That would be more than 24 hours of overtime on one day."** With the overtime already claimed for that day, this would go over 24 hours.
