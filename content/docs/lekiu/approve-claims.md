**Claim Approvals** lists the expense claims from your team. Owners and admins also record when an approved claim is paid.

## Who can do this

- **Owners and admins** see every claim. They can approve, reject and cancel, add a claim for any active employee, and **Mark paid** or **Undo paid**.
- **Managers** see only the claims of the people who report to them. They can approve and reject pending claims only.
- Everyone else sees "This page is for owners and admins of the workspace."

An admin cannot approve, reject or mark as paid their own claim. The workspace owner can.

## Who is told

When someone files a claim, their manager is told. If the person has no active manager, every owner and admin is told instead. The person who filed it is never told about their own claim.

## Steps

![The Claim Approvals screen with expense claims, receipts and Mark paid](/docs-media/lekiu/approve-claims.png)

_Shown from the sample workspace, where editing is switched off._

### Approve or reject

1. Open **Lekiu**, then **Financial Claims** under **Approvals**.
2. Find the claim in **Expense claims**. The **Details** column shows the receipt, if there is one, and how much was already approved or paid that year in the same category.
3. Click **Approve**. Or click **Reject**, type a note in the note box if you want, and click **Confirm reject**.

### Mark a claim as paid

1. On an approved claim, click **Mark paid**.
2. Check the date in the date box. It is the date paid, today unless you change it. It cannot be before the claim's date or after today.
3. Add a **Reference (optional)**, up to 100 characters.
4. Click **Confirm paid**. The status changes to **Paid**.

To take it back, click **Undo paid**, then **Confirm**. The claim goes back to approved.

### Add a claim for an employee

1. Click **Add claim for…**.
2. Choose the employee, then fill in the claim form as in [Make an expense claim](/docs/lekiu/make-an-expense-claim). The claim goes into the queue as pending.

### Cancel a claim

Click **Cancel** on the row, then **Confirm**. A paid claim cannot be cancelled until its paid mark is undone.

## Common problems

- **"Only an approved claim can be marked as paid."** Approve it first.
- **"That claim is marked as paid. Undo the paid mark first."** Undo paid before you cancel.
- **"You can't approve, reject or pay your own request. Ask the workspace owner or another admin."**
- **"That request has already been decided or cancelled."** Someone else decided it first.
