A new job is saved as a draft. Nobody outside your workspace sees it until you open it and turn on your careers page.

## Who can do this

Owners, admins and members. Viewers can see the Jobs screen but not the **Post a Job** button.

## Steps

1. Open **Lekir** and choose **Jobs**.
2. In **All jobs**, click **Post a Job**.
3. Under **Role**, fill in:
   - **Job title** (required), for example Sales Executive.
   - **Department**.
   - **Employment type**: **Full-time**, **Part-time**, **Contract** or **Internship**.
   - **Headcount**: how many people you are hiring.
4. Under **Where**, fill in **Location** and choose a **Work arrangement**: **On-site**, **Hybrid** or **Remote**, or leave it as **Not set**.
5. Under **Details**, fill in:
   - **Description**. You need one before you can open the job.
   - **Minimum salary** and **Maximum salary**, in RM a month. Leave them blank if you'd rather not say.
   - Tick **Show salary publicly** if your careers page may show the range.
   - **Closing date**: the last day to apply.
6. Click **Save**. You will see **Job saved as a draft.**
7. When you are ready for applications, click **Open** on the job's row.

## Common problems

- **Give the job a title.** The title is required. **Keep the title under 120 characters.**
- **Add a description before opening this job.** A job cannot be opened without a description.
- **Headcount must be at least 1.** Or **Headcount must be a whole number from 1 to 999.**
- **Enter an amount in ringgit, for example 3500.** Type a number. Commas and sen are fine.
- **Enter a monthly salary up to RM 10,000,000.**
- **Maximum salary can't be lower than the minimum.**
- **The closing date can't be in the past.**
- **Keep the department under 80 characters.** **Keep the location under 120 characters.** **Keep the description under 10,000 characters.**
