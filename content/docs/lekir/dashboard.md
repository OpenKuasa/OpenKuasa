The Dashboard shows how your recruiting is going, worked out from your jobs, applications and interviews.

## Who can do this

Everyone in the workspace can open the Dashboard: owners, admins, members and viewers. It only shows numbers. Nothing can be changed here.

## Steps

1. Open **Lekir** and choose **Dashboard**.
2. Read the top row:
   - **Time to hire**: the average number of days from applying to being hired.
   - **Applications**: all your applications.
   - **Offers out**: active applications at the Offer stage.
   - **Hires · last 30 days**: people hired in the last 30 days.
3. Read the cards below:
   - **Time to hire**: days to hire and to offer, month by month.
   - **Source effectiveness**: hires by where the candidate came from.
   - **Applications by job**, for all time.
   - **Hiring pipeline**: how many applications reached each stage, from Applied to Hired.
   - **Recent activity**: the latest applications, offers, hires and completed interviews.

A hire counts only while its application is still active. If a hired application is later marked withdrawn, it no longer counts as a hire.

## Common problems

- **Not available yet.** The **Offer-accept rate** card is not built yet.
- **The period menu and Export do nothing.** The **All time** menu and the **Export** button are switched off for now.
- **No offers or hires yet** or **Not enough history for a trend yet.** The time-to-hire chart needs offers or hires to work from.
- **Couldn't load your hiring data — please refresh.** The numbers could not be read. Refresh the page.
