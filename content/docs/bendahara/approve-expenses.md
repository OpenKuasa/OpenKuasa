An expense a member records waits as **Pending approval**. An owner or admin decides it, then records its payment.

## Who can do this

Only owners and admins can approve, reject, send back or mark an expense paid. Members and viewers cannot. In the demo workspace nothing can be changed.

## Before you start

To mark an expense paid you need a ringgit bank or cash account on [Banking](/docs/bendahara/banking).

## Steps

### Approve or reject

1. Open **Expenses** under **Purchases** in the Bendahara menu.
2. Choose **Pending approval** in the status list.
3. Open the row's menu and choose **Approve** or **Reject**, then confirm.

An approved expense is locked. It can then be paid, and only sent back or rejected while nothing has been paid. A rejected expense stays in the list as **Rejected**. Anyone who may edit can change it and send it again.

### Send back an approved expense

1. Open the row's menu on an approved expense with nothing paid, and choose **Send back**.
2. Type why it is sent back, then confirm with **Send back**.

It goes back to Pending to be corrected, and its entry in the books is reversed on that day. Approved again, it is a new entry.

### Mark an expense paid

1. Open the row's menu on an approved expense and choose **Mark paid**.
2. Choose the account it was **Paid from**, the **Date paid**, the **Method** and any **Reference**.
3. Check the **Amount paid (RM)**. Paying less leaves the rest for another payment.
4. Click **Mark paid**.

This records a payment voucher, paid now. It shows on [Payment vouchers](/docs/bendahara/payment-vouchers) and Banking. To undo a wrong one, void it on Payment Vouchers.

## Common problems

- **"This expense has a payment against it, paid or still to be paid, so it cannot be rejected."** The same applies to sending it back.
- **"This expense was changed after you opened it. Check it again, then approve or reject it."** Someone changed it meanwhile.
- **"Expenses are paid from a ringgit account. Choose a ringgit account."**
- **"That is more than is still to pay on this expense."**
- **"Only an owner or admin can approve or reject an expense. Ask one of them to do this."**
