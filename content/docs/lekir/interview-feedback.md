After an interview, you can record how it went. Feedback is a note for whoever decides. It does not move or reject the application.

## Who can do this

- **Add, edit or clear feedback:** owners, admins and members.
- **Read feedback:** everyone in the workspace. The **Feedback** column on the Interviews screen shows it, and the comments are under each interview in an application's details panel.

## Before you start

Feedback is for a completed interview. If the interview is still scheduled, use **Mark completed and add feedback**.

## Steps

1. Open **Lekir** and choose **Interviews**.
2. Click the **…** button on the interview's row.
3. Choose **Add feedback**. For a scheduled interview, choose **Mark completed and add feedback**.
4. Under **Rating**, choose 1 to 5. 1 is the lowest, 5 the highest.
5. Under **Recommendation**, choose **Advance**, **Hold** or **Reject**.
6. Under **Comments**, write what the interview showed that bears on the job. This is optional.
7. Click **Save feedback**.

To change it later, choose **Edit feedback**. To remove it, choose **Clear feedback** and confirm. The rating, the recommendation and the comments are removed, and the interview stays completed. Click **Keep feedback** to leave it.

Interview feedback is separate from the application's own star rating.

## Common problems

- **Choose a rating from 1 to 5.** **Choose a recommendation.**
- **Keep the comments under 2,000 characters.**
- **Feedback is for a completed interview. Mark this one completed first.**
- **This interview already has feedback. Open it to edit it.**
- **The feedback on this interview was changed a moment ago. Nothing was saved. Check it and try again.** Someone else saved feedback meanwhile. Click **Load the current feedback** to see it.
- **This interview has no feedback to clear.**
