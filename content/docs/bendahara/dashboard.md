The Dashboard is your financial overview. It is worked out from your books with the same sums as Financial Reports, so the two always agree.

## Who can do this

Everyone in the workspace can see the dashboard.

## Steps

![The finance Dashboard with cash position, revenue, expenses and net profit cards](/docs-media/bendahara/dashboard.png)

1. Open **Dashboard** under **Overview** in the Bendahara menu.
2. Choose the period at the top: **This month**, **This quarter** or **This year**. The quarter and the year follow the financial year end in [Accounting settings](/docs/bendahara/settings), which is December unless you change it.
3. Read the cards:
   - **Cash position**, with **Revenue**, **Expenses** and **Net profit** for the period;
   - **Cash flow**: closing balance and net cash over recent months;
   - **Expense breakdown** for the period;
   - **Profit & loss**: income against expense over recent months;
   - **Profit margin**: net profit as a share of revenue;
   - **AR / AP ageing**: what is receivable against what is payable;
   - **Bank & cash accounts** with their balances;
   - **Latest cash book activity**: money in and out, newest first. **View all** opens Banking.

If nothing is recorded yet, the dashboard says so and points you to Invoices and Banking.
