FX Revaluation shows your foreign-currency balances at a month end, in ringgit, at the closing rates. Posting it puts the gain or loss in your books. A revaluation is posted on the month end and reversed the next day.

## Who can do this

- Only owners and admins can post a revaluation. A member sees "An owner or admin posts revaluations." under the page title.
- Everyone in the workspace can see the screen and the posted revaluations.
- In the demo workspace nothing can be changed.

## Before you start

You need foreign-currency invoices, bills or bank accounts in your books. Without any, the screen says there is nothing to revalue. See [Foreign currency](/docs/bendahara/foreign-currency).

## Steps

1. Open **FX Revaluation** under **Analytics** in the Bendahara menu.
2. Choose the **Month end**.
3. Check the **Closing rates**. Each starts as Bank Negara Malaysia's 5 pm selling rate on or before the month end. You can type another rate.
4. Check the **Items**: for each one, the foreign balance, the rate, what it is carried at, what it is revalued at, the difference, earlier runs, and the gain or loss.
5. Click **Post revaluation**, then **Post FX revaluation** to confirm.

The posted revaluation is dated the month end and reversed the next day. It appears under **Posted revaluations**, where you can open its lines and **Export** them. You can also see it in the general ledger on [Journals](/docs/bendahara/journals).

## Common problems

- **"Choose the last day of a month, on or before today."**
- **"… is already posted. Month ends are revalued in order, so an earlier one cannot be revalued now."**
- **"The figures changed since this page worked them out: … Look at them again, then post."** A payment, a document or a rate may have arrived.
- **"Nothing to post: every foreign-currency balance at … is already at these rates."**
- **"Enter the closing rate for … before posting."** A currency has no rate yet. Type one in.
- **"A posted FX revaluation is not changed. Run the month end again to post a correction."**
- **"Only an owner or admin can post an FX revaluation."**
