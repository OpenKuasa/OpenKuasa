**My Payslips** shows your own payslips, newest first. A payslip appears once payroll for that month is marked as paid.

## Who can do this

Anyone in the workspace whose account is linked to their employee record. You only see your own paid payslips.

## Before you start

Your account must be linked to your employee record. See [Link an account to an employee record](/docs/lekiu/link-an-account).

## Steps

![The My Payslips screen with a payslip card for each paid month](/docs-media/lekiu/my-payslips.png)

1. Open **Lekiu**, then **My Payslips** under **General**.
2. Each month has its own card, with its **Net pay** under the month.
3. On a card, read **Basic pay** and any extra lines, such as overtime, then **Gross pay**.
4. Under **Deductions**, read EPF, SOCSO, EIS and PCB. Any other deductions HR added are listed under **Other deductions**.
5. At the bottom, read **Total deductions** and **Net pay**.

Your payslips are also listed on **My Documents**.

## Common problems

- **"No payslips yet. A payslip appears here once payroll for the month is marked as paid."** HR has not marked a run as paid for you yet.
- **A figure looks wrong**: ask an owner or admin. Only they can change a payslip, and only by undoing the paid run. They can only undo the newest month's run, so an older payslip can be changed only by undoing the later runs first, which deletes those runs' payslips and lines. That is a real cost, so it is not done lightly.
