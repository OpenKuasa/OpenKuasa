**Time-Off Approvals** lists short time-off requests from your team: the date, how long, and the reason.

## Who can do this

- **Owners and admins** see every request and can approve, reject and cancel.
- **Managers** see only the requests of the people who report to them, and can approve and reject pending ones.
- Everyone else sees "This page is for owners and admins of the workspace."

An admin cannot decide their own request. The workspace owner can.

## Steps

1. Open **Lekiu**, then **Time-Off** under **Approvals**.
2. Find the request in **Time-off requests**.
3. Click **Approve**. Or click **Reject**, type a **Note (optional)** and click **Confirm reject**.
4. To cancel a request (owners and admins), click **Cancel**, then **Confirm**.

There is no "add for someone" button on this screen.

## Common problems

- **"That request has already been decided or cancelled."** Someone else decided it first.
- **"You can't approve, reject or pay your own request. Ask the workspace owner or another admin."**
