On **HR Letters**, owners and admins write letters to employees, such as offers, confirmations and warnings. A letter stays a draft, unseen by the employee, until you issue it.

## Who can do this

Owners and admins, outside the demo workspace. Everyone else sees only the issued letters addressed to them.

## Before you start

The letterhead is made from your company details on your account's company page: the company name, registration number, address and logo.

## Steps

![The HR Letters screen with drafts and issued letters](/docs-media/lekiu/letters.png)

_Shown from the sample workspace, where editing is switched off._

### Start a letter

1. Open **Lekiu**, then **Letters** under **Approvals**.
2. Click **New letter**.
3. Choose the **Employee**. Only active employees are listed.
4. In **Start from**, pick **A blank letter** or a template. With a template, the draft opens with the employee's details filled in.
5. Click **Continue**. The draft is saved at once.

### Write and check it

1. Choose the **Type**: **Offer**, **Confirmation**, **Promotion**, **Warning**, **Contract renewal** or **Other**.
2. Fill in the **Title** and the **Letter**. Format with \*\*bold\*\*, \*italic\*, # headings, - bullets and 1. numbers, or the buttons above the text.
3. Click **Preview** to see the letter as it will print. Click **Edit** to go back.
4. If **Still to replace in this letter** shows, click each placeholder to find it, then replace or remove it.
5. To ask the employee to confirm they have read it, tick the box that starts with **Ask** and ends with **to confirm they have read it**.
6. Click **Save as draft** to finish later.

### Issue it

1. Click **Issue…**, then **Save and issue**. The employee can now see the letter.
2. On the list, a draft can also be issued with **Issue**, then **Issue** again.

Drafts come first in **Recent letters**. A draft can be edited or deleted. An issued letter can be viewed, printed, or pulled back with **Pull back to draft**. Pulling back hides it from the employee and clears any acknowledgement.

## Common problems

- **"The letter has no text yet."** Write the letter before issuing.
- **"The letter still has a placeholder in it. Replace it before issuing."** Look under **Still to replace in this letter**.
- **"That letter has been issued. Pull it back to draft on the Letters screen to change it."**
- **"Keep the letter to 10,000 characters."**
