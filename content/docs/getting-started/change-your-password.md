You change your password on the **Security** page. The same page lets you sign out of every other device.

## Who can do this

Every signed-in person can change their own password, whatever their role. Demo guests cannot: the demo has no password.

## Steps

### Change your password

1. Open the account menu (your name, top right) and choose **Change Password**. Or go to [Security](/account/security).
2. Under **Password**, enter your **Current password**.
3. Enter a **New password** of at least 8 characters, and type it again in **Confirm new password**.
4. Click **Update password**.

When you see **Password updated.**, your other devices are signed out. You stay signed in on this browser. OpenKuasa also sends you a notification that your password was changed.

### Sign out other devices

1. On the [Security](/account/security) page, find **Sessions**.
2. Next to **Other devices**, click **Sign out other devices**.

You see **Signed out of all other devices.** This browser stays signed in.

## Common problems

- **Your current password is incorrect.** Check the current password. If you have forgotten it, sign out and use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page.
- **New password must be at least 8 characters.** Choose a longer password.
- **The new passwords do not match.** Type the same new password in both boxes.
- **Password updated. Your other devices could not be signed out; use "Sign out other devices" below.** The password changed, but older sessions are still open. Click **Sign out other devices**.
