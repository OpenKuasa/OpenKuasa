Your careers page is a public job board for your company. People can read your open jobs and apply without signing in.

## Who can do this

Owners, admins and members. Viewers see the page's state but cannot change it: they see **You do not have permission to change this**.

## Before you start

The page lists only jobs that are **open** and have a **description**. Draft, paused and closed jobs are not shown. See [Post a job](/docs/lekir/post-a-job).

## Steps

1. Open **Lekir** and choose **Careers Page**.
2. Turn on **Public careers page**.
3. Read **Make your open jobs public?** and click **Make public**. Anyone with the link can then see your open jobs, and they can appear in search engines.
4. Click **Preview** to see the page as visitors do. It opens in a new tab.
5. Click **Copy link** to copy the page's address, ready to share.
6. Under **Page branding**, fill in a **Headline** (up to 80 characters) and a **Tagline** (up to 160 characters). Click **Save**. If you leave the headline blank, the page shows **Join our team**.
7. Under **Job listings**, click **Publish** to open a job, or **Unpublish** to close it.

To take the page down, turn **Public careers page** off. Your jobs are not changed.

## Common problems

- **A job is missing from the page.** Check that it is open and has a description. The Settings screen tells you how many open jobs have no description.
- **Turn the careers page on first.** **Preview** and **Copy link** only work while the page is on.
- **Applications closed.** A job past its closing date stays on the page, but shows **Applications closed**.
- **Add a description before opening this job.** **Publish** opens the job, so it needs a description first.
- **Not available yet.** **Page views**, **Applies**, **Conversion** and the charts on this screen are not built yet.
- **The demo workspace cannot have a public careers page.** In the demo, this screen shows a sample.
