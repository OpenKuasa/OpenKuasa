The Appointments screen lists your appointments with leads and contacts, soonest first, and shows how many people turned up.

## Who can do this

Owners, admins and members can book, edit, change the status of and delete appointments. Viewers can see them but not change them. The demo workspace is read-only.

## Steps

### Book an appointment

1. Open **Jebat** and choose **Appointments** in the side menu.
2. Click **New appointment**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Contact**: who the appointment is with (required, up to 120 characters).
   - **Kind**: what it is, for example a consultation or a follow-up call (required, up to 120 characters).
   - **When**: the date and time.
   - **Via**: optional, how you will meet, for example phone or a video call (up to 80 characters).
   - **Status**: it starts at **Scheduled**.
4. Click **Save**.

Times are shown in Malaysian time.

### Mark how it went

In the appointment's row, pick a new status from the **Status** list: **Scheduled**, **Completed**, **Cancelled** or **No-show**. It saves straight away.

### Reschedule or delete

- To change the time or details, click **Edit**, change them, and click **Save**.
- To remove it, click **Delete**, then **Confirm?**. Click **Cancel** to keep it.

### Read the figures

- **Total** counts every appointment.
- **Upcoming** counts scheduled appointments that have not happened yet.
- **Completed** counts the completed ones.
- **Show-rate** is completed appointments divided by completed plus no-shows. It shows **—** until one is completed or a no-show.

## Common problems

- **"Pick a valid date and time."** Fill in **When** before you save.
- **"That appointment was not found."** Someone else deleted it. Refresh the page.
