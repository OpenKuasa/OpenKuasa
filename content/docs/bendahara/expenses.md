Expenses holds what was spent on the business. An expense waits as **Pending approval** until an owner or admin approves it. Once approved, it is in your books and can be paid.

## Who can do this

- Owners, admins and members can record expenses, and edit or delete them while they are pending or rejected.
- When an owner or admin saves an expense, it is approved at once.
- Only owners and admins can approve, reject, send back or mark an expense paid. See [Approve and pay expenses](/docs/bendahara/approve-expenses).
- Viewers can only look. In the demo workspace nothing can be changed.

## Steps

![The Expenses screen with totals, charts and the expenses table](/docs-media/bendahara/expenses.png)

### Record an expense

1. Open **Expenses** under **Purchases** in the Bendahara menu.
2. Click **New expense**.
3. Set the **Date** and choose the **Category**.
4. Under **Vendor**, choose the contact who was paid, or choose **Not a contact** and type the **Vendor name**.
5. Enter the **Amount (RM), SST included**.
6. If the amount has SST in it, enter the **SST inside it (RM)**.
7. Add any **Notes**.
8. Click **Save expense**.

The line above the button tells you what happens when you save. As a member: "This expense waits as Pending approval until an owner or admin approves it. Until then anyone who may edit can change or delete it." As an owner or admin: "An expense you save is approved at once: you are an owner or admin."

### Change or delete an expense

1. Find the expense in the **Expenses** table. Use the category and status lists, or search.
2. Open the row's menu and choose **Edit** or **Delete**.
3. A rejected expense can be edited and sent again. Saving it sends it for approval again.

An approved expense is locked. It cannot be changed or deleted.

## Common problems

- **"Say who was paid: choose a contact or type a name."**
- **"Enter the amount as a number, without commas or RM: 1200.50."** Type plain numbers, with up to two decimal places.
- **"The SST cannot be more than the amount."**
- **"This expense has been approved, so it can no longer be changed. …"** An owner or admin can reject it first, while no payment stands against it.
- **"This expense has been in the books, so it is kept and cannot be deleted."** An expense that was ever approved stays.
- **"This expense has a voided payment against it, so it is kept."**
