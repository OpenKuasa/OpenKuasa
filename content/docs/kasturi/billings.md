Billings shows your sales invoices and collections inside Kasturi. The invoices come from Bendahara. Nothing is changed here: invoices are created and edited in Bendahara.

## Who can do this

Everyone in the workspace can open Billings. Creating and changing invoices happens in Bendahara, under its own rules.

## Steps

1. Open **Kasturi** and choose **Billings**.
2. Read the figures at the top:
   - **Billed in** this month: invoices dated this month that have been issued.
   - **Outstanding**: what is still owed on sent and overdue invoices.
   - **Overdue**: the part of what is owed that is past its due date.
   - **Received**: money received against issued invoices.
3. Look at the charts: **Billed by month**, **Collection rate** (received out of everything billed), **Invoices by status** and **Top customers**.
4. Check **Recent invoices** for the newest invoices, with the customer, the linked deal, the dates, the total, the balance and the status: **Draft**, **Sent**, **Overdue**, **Paid** or **Void**.
5. To create or change an invoice, click **Manage invoices** or **View all**. They open Bendahara's Invoices screen.

![The Billings screen with invoice figures](/docs-media/kasturi/billings.png)

### Link an invoice to a deal

The **Deal** column shows the deal an invoice belongs to. To link one, edit the deal on the Deals screen and tick it under **Invoices**.

## Common problems

- **No invoices yet. Create one on Bendahara's Invoices screen.**: the workspace has no invoices.
- **No invoices posted yet**: invoices that are still drafts do not count as billed.
- Each invoice's total and balance are in its own currency. Every sum on the page is in ringgit.
- **Couldn't load your invoices — please refresh**: reload the page.
