The **Timesheet** shows hours worked per day for one week, Monday to Sunday, and how many of them were billable.

## Who can do this

- **Anyone linked to an employee record** can type their own hours for this week and last week, for days that have already come.
- **Owners and admins** can type hours for any active employee, for any day that has come.
- **Managers** also see the rows of the people who report to them, to read.

## Before you start

Your account must be linked to your employee record.

## Steps

![The Timesheet screen with hours by employee and the weekly timesheet](/docs-media/lekiu/timesheet.png)

1. Open **Lekiu**, then **Timesheet** under **Attendance**.
2. Use the arrows or **This week** to pick the week.
3. In your row, type the hours in the box for each day. Type the billable hours in the small box under it.
4. Leave a day empty to clear it.
5. Click **Save hours**. To throw your changes away instead, click **Discard**.

Hours go from 0 to 24 in steps of half an hour. Billable hours cannot be more than the hours.

## Common problems

- **"You can record your own hours for this week and last week only. Ask an owner or admin to correct an older week."**
- **"Hours can't be recorded for a day that hasn't happened yet."**
- **"Hours must be 0 to 24 in steps of half an hour, and billable hours no more than the hours."**
- **"You can only record your own hours."**
