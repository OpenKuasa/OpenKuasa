Appointments lists the meetings, calls and visits you book with your contacts. Kasturi and Jebat share the same appointments, so one booked in either shows in both.

## Who can do this

- Everyone in the workspace can see appointments.
- Owners, admins and members can book, change, and delete them. Viewers can only read.

## Steps

### Book an appointment

1. Open **Kasturi** and choose **Appointments**.
2. Click **New appointment**.
3. Fill in **Contact** (the person's name), **Kind** and **When**. All three are needed.
4. Add **Via** if you want.
5. Choose the **Status**. A new appointment starts as **Scheduled**.
6. Click **Save**.

### Change the status

In the **Status** column, pick **Scheduled**, **Completed**, **Cancelled** or **No-show**. It saves straight away.

### Reschedule or delete

- Click **Edit** on the row, change **When** or anything else, and click **Save**.
- Click **Delete**, then **Confirm?** to remove it.

### Read the figures

- **Total**: every appointment.
- **Upcoming**: scheduled ones still to come.
- **Completed**: ones marked completed.
- **Show-rate**: completed out of completed and no-shows.

Appointments also show on the Calendar, except cancelled ones.

## Common problems

- **Pick a valid date and time.**: choose a date and time in **When**.
- The **Contact** field is typed, not picked from your contacts.
- **You do not have permission to make changes here.**: viewers and the demo workspace cannot change appointments.
