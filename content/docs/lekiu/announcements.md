**Announcements** are company-wide posts that everyone in the workspace reads. New posts you have not read are marked, and the **Announcements** menu item shows how many are unread.

## Who can do this

- **Everyone** in the workspace reads published announcements.
- **Owners and admins** also see drafts, and can write, publish, edit, pull back and delete, outside the demo workspace.

## Steps

![The Announcements screen with a draft and published posts](/docs-media/lekiu/announcements.png)

### Post an announcement

1. Open **Lekiu**, then **Announcements** under **General**.
2. Click **New Announcement**.
3. Fill in the **Title** (up to 200 characters), choose the **Category** (**General**, **Holiday**, **Benefits**, **Strategy** or **Policy**) and write the **Text** (up to 5,000 characters).
4. Click **Publish** to post it to the whole workspace, or **Save as draft** to finish later.

### Change one

- **Edit** a published post and click **Save changes**. It stays published and is marked as edited.
- **Publish** a draft, then **Publish** again to confirm.
- **Pull back to draft** hides a published post. Confirm with **Pull back**.
- **Delete**, then **Delete** again, removes it.
