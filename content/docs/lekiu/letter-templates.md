A template is a starting point for a letter. When you start a letter from it, the placeholders are filled with that employee's details.

## Who can do this

Owners and admins, outside the demo workspace.

## Steps

1. Open **Lekiu**, then **Letters** under **Approvals**.
2. In the **Templates** card, click **Add template**, or **Edit** on an existing one.
3. Fill in the **Template name** (up to 120 characters) and choose the **Type**.
4. Choose the **Language**: **English** or **Bahasa Malaysia**. Dates and the salary are written in this language when a letter is started from the template.
5. Fill in the **Title** and the **Letter text**.
6. Under **Placeholders**, click one to put it where your cursor is, in the title or the text. The placeholders are `{employee_name}`, `{employee_no}`, `{designation}`, `{department}`, `{join_date}`, `{company_name}`, `{today}` and `{salary}`.
7. Click **Save template**.

Changing a template does not change letters already started from it. To delete one, click **Remove**, then **Remove** again.

Any placeholder that cannot be filled for an employee is left in the letter for you to replace before issuing.

## Common problems

- **"There is already a template with that name."**
- **"That is 50 templates already. Remove one first."**
