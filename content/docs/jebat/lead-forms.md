A lead form is a simple page people fill in to send you their details. Each person who sends it becomes a Kasturi contact and shows up in your [Lead Funnel](/docs/jebat/lead-funnel). The same forms also show in Kasturi under **Lead Forms**.

## Who can do this

Owners, admins and members can create, edit, activate, pause and delete forms. Viewers can see the forms but not change them. The demo workspace is read-only, and its forms are samples with no public page.

## Steps

### Create a form

1. Open **Jebat** and choose **Lead Forms** in the side menu.
2. Click **Create new form**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Name** (required, up to 120 characters), for example "Raya promo signup".
   - **Category** (optional, up to 60 characters). Pick a suggestion or type your own.
   - **Short name**: a label for your own use, made from the name until you type your own. Use 2 to 60 lower-case letters, numbers and hyphens, such as `raya-promo`.
   - **Status**: **Draft**, **Active** or **Paused**.
   - **Consent sentence**: the sentence visitors tick to agree before they can send the form, up to 300 characters. `{workspace}` in it becomes your workspace's name.
4. Click **Save form**.

![The Your forms table in the demo, with each form's short name, status, views and contacts](/docs-media/jebat/lead-forms-table.png)

The demo's forms are samples, so the picture shows only each form's short name under **Public link**. In your own workspace, an active form also shows its link with **Copy link** and **Open**.

### Share the link

1. Make sure the form is **Active**. If it is not, open the **⋯** menu on its row and choose **Activate**.
2. In the **Public link** column, click **Copy link**, then paste the link wherever you want people to find it. Click **Open** to see the page yourself.

The public page asks for **Name**, **Email**, **Phone (optional)** and **Message (optional)**, and has a tick box beside your consent sentence. **Views** counts visits to the page; visits by people in your workspace are not counted. **Contacts** counts the forms sent.

### Pause, edit or delete a form

- To stop taking responses, open the **⋯** menu and choose **Pause**. Visitors then see "This form is not accepting responses."
- To change a form, open the **⋯** menu, choose **Edit**, change it, and click **Save changes**.
- To delete it, open the **⋯** menu, choose **Delete**, then click **Delete form**. Its public link stops working and its submissions are deleted, but the contacts they made stay. This cannot be undone.

### Find a form

Search by name or short name in **Search form title…**, or narrow the list with **All categories** and **All statuses**. Click **Clear filters** to see every form again.

### Read the figures

- **Total forms** counts your forms.
- **Total leads** counts everything sent through all your forms.
- **Conversion** is what was sent divided by the views.
- **New today** and **Submissions over time** count what came in today and over the last 14 days.
- **Top forms by contacts** shows the forms with the most contacts.

## Common problems

- **"Activate this form to get its link."** Only an active form has a public link.
- **"Another form already uses that short name."** Pick a different short name.
- **"Use only lower-case letters, numbers and hyphens in the short name, such as raya-promo."** The short name has other characters in it, or is shorter than 2 characters.
- **"Enter a name for the form."** or **"Enter the sentence visitors tick to agree."** A required field is empty.
- **"Keep the consent sentence to 300 characters or fewer."** Shorten the sentence.
- **"That form no longer exists."** Someone else deleted it. Refresh the page.
- **"This form is receiving a lot of responses right now. Try again in a minute."** Visitors may see this when many people send the form at once.
