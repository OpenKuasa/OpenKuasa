The Calendar puts your appointments, open follow-ups and expected deal closes on one month grid. It shows what is already in Kasturi; you add things on their own screens.

## Who can do this

Everyone in the workspace can open the Calendar.

## Steps

1. Open **Kasturi** and choose **Calendar**.
2. Read the figures at the top: **Today**, **Next 7 days**, the month on show, and **Next 30 days**.
3. Move between months with the previous and next buttons. Click **Today** to come back to this month.
4. Each day shows up to three entries, and **+N more** if there are others. The colours are:
   - **Appointment**: appointments that are not cancelled, with the time.
   - **Follow-up**: open follow-ups that have a due date.
   - **Deal closing**: open deals on their expected close date.
5. Click an entry to go to its screen: Appointments, Contacts or Deals.
6. **Upcoming** lists the next entries in the coming 30 days.

![The Kasturi Calendar month view](/docs-media/kasturi/calendar.png)

## Common problems

- A follow-up with no due date is not on the Calendar. Add a due date to it on the Contacts screen.
- A deal with no expected close date, or one that is won or lost, is not on the Calendar.
- Dates and times are in Kuala Lumpur time.
- **Couldn't load your calendar — please refresh**: reload the page.
