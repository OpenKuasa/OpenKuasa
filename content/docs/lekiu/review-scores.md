On **Review Scores**, owners and admins write performance reviews. A review is saved as a draft. The employee sees it only after you release it.

## Who can do this

- **Owners and admins** write, edit, release, pull back and delete reviews, outside the demo workspace.
- **Everyone else** sees only their own released reviews.

## Steps

![The Review Scores screen with ratings and scores by department](/docs-media/lekiu/review-scores.png)

_Shown from the sample workspace, where editing is switched off._

### Write a review

1. Open **Lekiu**, then **Review Scores** under **Performance**.
2. Click **New review**.
3. Choose the **Employee** and the **Period**, such as H1, H2 or FY of a year. A period already reviewed for that person shows **(already reviewed)**.
4. Under **Scores out of 5**, give each competency a score: **Delivery**, **Teamwork**, **Ownership** and **Communication**. The overall score is worked out when you save.
5. Choose the **Rating**: **Exceeds expectations**, **Meets expectations** or **Below expectations**.
6. Add a **Comment (optional)**, up to 2,000 characters.
7. Click **Save as draft**.

### Release it

1. On the review's row, click **Release**, then **Release** again.

To change a released review, click **Pull back to draft**, then **Pull back**. Only a draft can be edited or deleted.

## Common problems

- **"There is already a review for that period."** Each person has one review per period.
- **"That review has been released. Pull it back to draft on the Review Scores screen to change it."**
- **"Choose whether it exceeds, meets or is below expectations."** Pick a rating.
