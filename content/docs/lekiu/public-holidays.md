**Public Holidays** is your workspace's holiday calendar for the year. A public holiday is not a working day: nobody is marked absent on it, and it is not counted in leave.

## Who can do this

- **Everyone** in the workspace can see the calendar.
- **Owners and admins** can add, edit and remove holidays, outside the demo workspace.

## Steps

![The Public Holidays screen with the year's holiday calendar](/docs-media/lekiu/public-holidays.png)

### See the holidays

1. Open **Lekiu**, then **Public Holidays** under **Approvals**.
2. Read **Holidays in** the year, **Next holiday** and **This month**, the **By type** chart and **Coming up**.
3. The calendar table lists every holiday with its date, day, scope and state. Past holidays are dimmed.

### Add a holiday

1. Click **Add holiday**.
2. Type the **Name** (up to 120 characters) and pick the **Date**.
3. Click **Save**.

### Change or remove a holiday

- Click **Edit** on its row, change the name or date, and click **Save**.
- Click **Remove**, then **Confirm**.
