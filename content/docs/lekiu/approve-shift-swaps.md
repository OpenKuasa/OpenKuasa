When two employees agree to swap shifts, the swap comes to **Shift Swaps** for a decision. Approving it exchanges the two shifts on the roster.

## Who can do this

- **Owners and admins** see every swap and can approve or reject it.
- **Managers** see swaps between two people who report to them, and can decide those.
- Someone who is one of the two people in a swap cannot decide it. Their row says "You are in this swap: someone else decides it". The workspace owner can decide their own.
- Everyone else sees "This page is for owners and admins of the workspace."

Shift swaps are not part of the demo workspace.

## Steps

1. Open **Lekiu**, then **Shift Swaps** under **Approvals**.
2. Look at **Waiting for a decision**. These are swaps both people have agreed to. Each row shows **Asked by**, **Gives**, **Swaps with** and **Takes**.
3. Click **Approve**. Or click **Reject**, type a note in the note box if you want, and click **Confirm reject**.

**Waiting for the colleague** lists swaps the colleague has not answered yet. There is nothing to decide there. **Recent** lists swaps that were decided, declined, cancelled or expired.

## Common problems

- **"The roster changed since this swap was asked for, so it cannot be approved. Reject it, and ask again if it still makes sense."**
- **"You are one of the two people in this swap, so someone else has to decide it."**
- **"That swap has already moved on. Refresh the page to see where it stands."**
- **"Only owners, admins and a manager of both people can decide a shift swap."**
