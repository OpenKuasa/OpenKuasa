Deals is your sales pipeline. Each deal belongs to a contact and sits in a stage on the board. You move it along until it is won or lost.

## Who can do this

- Everyone in the workspace can see the deals, the figures and the **Daily Report**.
- Owners, admins and members can add, edit, move, lose, reopen and delete deals. Viewers can only read.

## Before you start

- A deal needs a contact. Add the contact first on the Contacts screen.
- A new workspace gets a starting pipeline called **Sales pipeline** with the stages Lead, Qualified, Proposal, Negotiation and Won. It is set up the first time someone who can edit deals opens this screen.

## Steps

### Add a deal

1. Open **Kasturi** and choose **Deals**.
2. Click **New Deal**.
3. Fill in **Title** and choose a **Contact**. Both are needed.
4. Choose the **Stage**, and add **Value (RM)**, **Tag** and **Expected close date** if you have them.
5. Click **Save deal**. The form clears so you can add the next one. Click **Close** when you are done.

You become the owner of the deals you add. You can also start a deal from a contact: choose **Create deal** in the contact's **⋯** menu.

### Move a deal

- Drag the deal's card to another stage on the **Pipeline board**.
- Or open the card's **⋯** menu, choose **Move to**, and pick the stage.

A deal moved into the stage called **Won** counts as won. Moving it out of **Won** makes it open again.

### Mark a deal as lost, or reopen it

1. Open the card's **⋯** menu and choose **Mark as lost**.
2. Type a **Reason (optional)** if you want. If your workspace requires a reason, the field is called **Reason** and must be filled in.
3. Click **Mark as lost**. The deal leaves the open deals and counts against the win rate.
4. To bring it back, open the card's **⋯** menu and choose **Reopen**.

### Edit or delete a deal

1. Open the card's **⋯** menu and choose **Edit**. Change what you need and click **Save changes**.
2. When you edit a deal, you can also tick the Bendahara **Invoices** that belong to it.
3. To delete, choose **Delete**, then **Delete deal**. The deal's activities and notes are deleted too. This cannot be undone.

### Find deals and read the figures

- Pick the pipeline, the owner (**All owners** or one person) and the view: **Open and won**, **Open**, **Won**, **Lost** or **All deals**.
- Search with **Search deals across all stages…**.
- The figures at the top (**Pipeline value**, **Open deals**, **Avg deal size**, **Win rate**) are for the pipeline on show.
- Click **Daily Report** for today's figures: **Created today**, **Won today**, **Open pipeline value** and **Closing in 7 days**.

## Common problems

- **Enter a title for the deal.**, **Choose a contact.** or **Choose a stage.**: fill in the field it names.
- **Enter the value as a number of ringgit, such as 18000.**: type numbers only, with up to two decimal places.
- **Keep the title to 200 characters or fewer.** or **Keep the reason to 200 characters or fewer.**: shorten the text.
- **Enter a valid expected close date.**: pick a real date.
- **This workspace requires a reason to mark a deal lost. Enter one and try again.**: your workspace has turned on the lost-reason rule in Settings. Type a reason.
- **Could not move the deal. Please try again.**: the card goes back. Try the move again.
- **This pipeline has no stages yet, so it cannot hold deals.**: add stages under **Manage pipelines**.
- The board and its figures cover your 500 most recent deals.
