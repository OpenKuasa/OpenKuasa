You sign in with the email and password you chose when you signed up.

## Who can do this

Anyone with an OpenKuasa account. Demo guests have no password and do not need to sign in.

## Steps

### Sign in

1. On the OpenKuasa website, click **Login**.
2. Enter your **Email / Username** and **Password**.
3. Leave **Remember me** ticked to stay signed in on this device. Untick it on a shared computer: you are then signed out when the browser closes.
4. Click **Sign in**.

If you turned on two-step sign-in, OpenKuasa now asks for a code from your authenticator app. See [Turn on two-step sign-in](/docs/getting-started/two-step-sign-in).

**Sign in with Google** is not available yet. Clicking it shows **Google sign-in isn't enabled yet.**

### Reset a forgotten password

1. On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your **Email** and click **Send reset link**.
3. Open the link in the email. It takes you to a page where you set a new password.
4. Enter a **New password** of at least 8 characters and click **Update password**.

You are signed in, and your other devices are signed out. OpenKuasa also sends you a notification that your password was reset.

For safety, the page always says **If that email has an account, a reset link is on its way.** It does not tell you whether the email has an account.

## Common problems

- **Incorrect email or password.** Check both and try again, or reset your password.
- **Your reset link has expired. Request a new one.** Ask for a new link with **Forgot password?**
- **That link is invalid or has already been used.** Each reset link works once. Ask for a new one.
- **This page only works straight after opening a reset link. Request a new reset link and try again.** Open the newest link from your email, then set the password straight away.
- **Your session expired. Please sign in again.** Sign in again.
