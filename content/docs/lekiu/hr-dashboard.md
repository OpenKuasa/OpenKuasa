The HR Dashboard shows people trends across your workspace in one place. It only shows information; you cannot change anything here.

## Who can do this

Everyone in the workspace can open it. Owners and admins see every card. Everyone else sees "Shown to HR admins" on the team cards: **Clocked in so far today**, **Payroll, latest run**, **Attendance trend**, **Leave this month**, **Joiners** and **Payroll cost**.

If people report to you, you also see a **My team** card.

## Steps

1. Open **Lekiu**, then **Dashboard** under **General**.
2. Read the top row: **Headcount**, **Clocked in so far today** and **Payroll, latest run**.
3. If you are a manager, read **My team**. It shows **Reporting to you**, who is **Away today**, and what is **Waiting for you**. Click an item under **Waiting for you** to open that approval screen.
4. Read the charts: **Attendance trend**, **Leave this month**, **Headcount by department**, **Joiners** and **Payroll cost**.

## Common problems

- **Export does nothing**: **Export** is not built yet. It shows "Available in a later update".
