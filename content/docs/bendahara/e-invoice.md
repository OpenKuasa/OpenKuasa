Sending invoices to LHDN MyInvois from Bendahara is not available yet. The **e-Invoice LHDN** screen shows **Coming soon**. Nothing on it sends anything to LHDN.

## Who can do this

Everyone in the workspace can open the screen. There is nothing to do on it yet.

## Steps

Until sending is built, submit your invoices on the MyInvois portal yourself. Then record what LHDN gave you on the invoice in Bendahara:

1. Open **Invoices** under **Sales** and find the posted invoice.
2. Open its row menu and choose **Set e-Invois**.
3. Choose the **Status**: **Pending**, **Validated**, **Rejected** or **None**, and type the **LHDN UUID**.
4. Click **Save**.

This only records what you did on the portal. Nothing is submitted from Bendahara. Owners, admins and members can record it; it can only be recorded once the invoice is posted.

The MyInvois integration, auto-submitting validated invoices and e-Invois alerts are listed as **Coming soon** in [Accounting settings](/docs/bendahara/settings).
