A landing page is a public page that puts one of your lead forms in front of visitors. It has a headline, a short text and the form. You can also show the AI chatbot on it.

## Who can do this

- Everyone in the workspace can see the pages and their counts.
- Owners, admins and members can add, edit and delete pages. Viewers can only read.

## Before you start

A page shows one of your lead forms. Make the form first on the Lead Forms screen. A published page needs a form.

## Steps

### Make a page

1. Open **Kasturi** and choose **Landing Page**.
2. Click **New page**.
3. Type a **Name**. Only you see this.
4. Choose the **Lead form** the page shows. Responses go to this form, and its contacts, as usual.
5. Type the **Headline**. Add a **Subheading**, **Text** and a **Button label** if you want them. The button says **Send** unless you change it.
6. Choose the **Status**: **Draft (only you can see it)** or **Published (anyone with the link)**.
7. Tick **Show the AI chatbot on this page** if you want visitors to be able to ask your bot questions.
8. Check the **Preview**, then click **Save page**.

### Share a page

Under **My pages**, use the copy-link button on the page's row to copy its link, or the open button to see it in a new tab.

### Read the counts

The page list shows each page's **Views**, **Leads** and **Conversion** (leads out of views). The figures at the top add up all your pages.

### Edit or delete a page

- Click the page's edit button, change it, and click **Save changes**.
- Click its delete button, then **Delete page**. Its link stops working and its counts are lost. The lead form and its contacts are kept. This cannot be undone.

## Common problems

- **Choose a lead form before publishing: a published page needs one to show.**: pick a form, or save the page as a draft.
- **Form not active**: the page is published, but its lead form is not active. Visitors see "page not available" until you activate the form.
- **Enter a name for the page.** or **Enter a headline.**: fill in the field it names.
- **Keep the headline to 160 characters or fewer.** or **Keep the text to 4000 characters or fewer.**: shorten the text.
- The chatbot only appears when the workspace has its own AI key.
