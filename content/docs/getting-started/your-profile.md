Your profile holds your own details. Your name and photo show in the menus and on the **Team** page.

## Who can do this

Every signed-in person can change their own profile, whatever their role. In the demo workspace the profile is read-only.

## Steps

### Open your profile

Open the account menu (your name, top right) and click **Profile & Preferences** at the top. Or go to [My profile](/account/profile).

### Add or change your photo

1. Under **Profile photo**, click **Upload photo** (or **Change photo** if you already have one).
2. Choose a JPG, PNG or WebP image, up to 2MB.

You see **Photo updated.** Until you add a photo, your initials are shown. To take the photo off, click **Remove**.

### Change your details

1. Under **Personal details**, fill in **Full name**, **Phone** and **Job title**, and choose your **Timezone**.
2. Under **Preferences**, choose a **Language**. It is saved for later: the app is in English for now.
3. Turn **Email me product updates** on or off.
4. Click **Save changes**.

You see **Profile saved.**

### Change your sign-in email

1. Under **Sign-in email**, enter the **New email**.
2. Enter your **Current password**. If you use two-step sign-in, also enter the **Authenticator code** from your app.
3. Click **Send confirmation link**.
4. Open the link that arrives at the new address.

Nothing changes until you open that link. Until then you keep signing in with your current email, and the page shows **Waiting for confirmation of** the new address.

## Common problems

- **Please enter your name.** **Full name** cannot be empty.
- **Please choose a JPG, PNG or WebP image.** or **Photos can be up to 2MB.** Pick a different file.
- **That is already your sign-in email.** Enter a different address.
- **Your current password is incorrect.** Check your password and try again.
- **Too many emails sent. Please wait a few minutes and try again.** Wait a few minutes before asking for another link.
- **The demo workspace is read-only. Sign up to save changes.** The demo profile cannot be changed.
