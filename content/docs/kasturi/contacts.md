Contacts holds all your leads and customers. Each contact can have tags, a lead score, a person in charge and follow-ups.

## Who can do this

- Everyone in the workspace can see contacts.
- Owners, admins and members can add, edit and delete contacts and follow-ups. Viewers can only read.

## Steps

### Add a contact

1. Open **Kasturi** and choose **Contacts**.
2. Click **Add Contact**, then **Add one contact**.
3. Fill in **First name** and **Email**. These two are needed.
4. Add the rest if you have it: **Last name**, **Phone**, **Company**, **Country code**, **Status**, **Lead score** (0 to 100) and **Tags** (separated by commas). **Billing customer** links the contact to the Bendahara customer it is invoiced through.
5. Click **Save contact**. The form clears so you can add the next one. Click **Close** when you are done.

You become the person in charge (PIC) of the contacts you add.

![The Contacts screen with the contacts table](/docs-media/kasturi/contacts.png)

### Edit or delete a contact

1. Click the **⋯** button at the end of the contact's row.
2. Choose **Edit**, change what you need, and click **Save changes**.
3. To delete, choose **Delete**, then **Delete contact**. The contact's deals, notes and follow-ups are deleted too. This cannot be undone.

From the same menu, **Create deal** opens a new deal for that contact.

### Add a follow-up

1. Under the contact's email, click **+ Add follow-up**.
2. Type what to follow up on, and pick a due date if you want one.
3. Click **Save**.
4. When it is done, click **Done** beside it.

Follow-ups with a due date also show on the Calendar.

### Find contacts

- Use the view menu to choose **Active contacts**, **New leads**, **Contacted**, **Qualified**, **Customers**, **Archived** or **All contacts**. **Active contacts** leaves out archived ones.
- Use **Tags** to show only contacts with the tags you tick.
- Use **Follow-up** to show **With a follow-up** or **Overdue only**.
- Click **Filter** to search by name, email, company, phone or tag, and to narrow by **Lead score**, **PIC** or **Country**.
- Use **Columns** to hide or show columns. Your choice is kept in this browser.

## Common problems

- **Enter a first name.** or **Enter a valid email.**: both are needed for every contact.
- **Use a two-letter country code, such as MY.**: type the code, not the country name.
- **Use at most 10 tags per contact.** or **Keep each tag to 30 characters or fewer.**: separate tags with commas.
- **Enter what to follow up on.** or **Keep the follow-up under 200 characters.**: check the follow-up text.
- The screen loads your 500 most recent contacts. Views, filters and search cover only those.
- **Archived** can only be chosen when you edit a contact, not when you add one.
