**Team Attendance** shows one day at a time, with a row for each employee: status, clock-in, clock-out and hours.

## Who can do this

- **Owners and admins** see everyone and can correct any day's record.
- **Managers** see the people who report to them, to read only.
- Everyone else sees "This page is for owners and admins of the workspace."

## Steps

### Look at a day

1. Open **Lekiu**, then **Attendance** under **Management**.
2. The screen opens on today. Use the arrows for the previous or next day, or pick a date in **Day** and click **Show**.
3. Read the counts: **Present**, **Late**, **Absent**, **On leave** and **No clock-out**. The **Notes** column marks **No clock-out**, **Still in** and **Corrected**.

### Correct a record

1. On the employee's row, click **Correct**.
2. Choose the **Status**: **Present**, **Late**, **Absent** or **On leave**.
3. For present or late, set the **Clock-in** time, and the **Clock-out (optional)** time.
4. Add a **Note (optional)**, up to 500 characters. The employee sees it on **My Attendance**.
5. Click **Confirm**. The row shows **Corrected**.

## Common problems

- **"Some of that was not accepted. A date or time may be in the future, a time may not be a time of day, a clock-out may not be after the clock-in, or a name or note may be too long or empty. Check it and try again."**
- **"Nobody reports to you"**: as a manager, you will see rows once people have you in **Reports to**.
