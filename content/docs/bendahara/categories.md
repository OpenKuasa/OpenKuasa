Categories sort your spending and income. Expense categories are offered on new expenses, supplier bills and payment vouchers. Income categories are offered on new cash receipts. Each category has its own account in the Chart of Accounts.

## Who can do this

Only owners and admins can add, rename, archive or restore categories. Only they see the **Categories** button. In the demo workspace nothing can be changed.

## Steps

### Add a category

1. Open **Expenses** under **Purchases** in the Bendahara menu.
2. Click **Categories**.
3. Type the **New category** name and choose its **Kind**: **Expense** or **Income**. The kind cannot be changed afterwards.
4. Click **Add category**.

### Rename, archive or restore a category

1. In the **Categories** card, open the category's row menu.
2. Choose **Rename** and type the new name, then click **Save name**. Everything filed under it shows the new name. Its account in the Chart of Accounts is renamed too, unless that one was renamed there.
3. Or choose **Archive** and confirm with **Archive category**. It is no longer offered on new forms, and its account is archived with it. What is already filed under it keeps it.
4. An archived category shows **Restore** in its menu.

Categories are never deleted.

## Common problems

- **"There is already an expense category called …"** Each name is used once per kind.
- **"An archived expense category is already called … Restore it from its row rather than adding it again."**
- **"Invoices post to this category, so it is not archived. It can be renamed."** The category marked **Invoices post here** stays.
- **"A bill with no category goes to this one, so it is not archived. It can be renamed."** The category marked **Bills default here** stays.
- **"Only an owner or admin can add or change categories. Ask one of them to do this."**
