Ad Studio keeps a list of your ad campaigns, with what each one spent and the leads it brought in. You type these figures in yourself.

## Who can do this

Owners, admins and members can add, edit, pause, resume and delete campaigns. Viewers can see the campaigns but not change them. The demo workspace is read-only.

## Steps

### Add a campaign

1. Open **Jebat** and choose **Ad Studio** in the side menu.
2. In the **Campaigns** card, click **New Campaign**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Campaign name** (required, up to 120 characters).
   - **Channel**: **WhatsApp**, **Facebook**, **Instagram** or **TikTok**.
   - **Status**: **Active** or **Paused**.
   - **Spend (RM)**: what the campaign has spent so far.
   - **Leads**: how many leads it has brought in.
4. Click **Save**.

The **CPL** (cost per lead) column is worked out for you: spend divided by leads. It shows **—** while a campaign has no leads.

### Update, pause or delete a campaign

- To change its figures, click **Edit** on its row, change them, and click **Save**.
- To mark it paused, click **Pause**. Click **Resume** to mark it active again.
- To remove it, click **Delete**, then **Confirm?**. Click **Cancel** to keep it.

### Read the figures

- **Active campaigns**, **Ad spend** and **Cost / lead** at the top are counted from all your campaigns.
- **Spend by channel** and **Leads by channel** split them by channel.

## Common problems

- **"Reach", "Spend & leads", "Ad funnel" and "Budget used" say "Not available yet".** These figures are not built for your own workspace yet. Only the demo shows sample figures in them.
- **Connecting a Meta account.** Ad Studio does not connect to Meta yet, so ads are not launched or synced from here. The **Connect Meta**, **Login with Facebook** and **Audiences** buttons do not do anything yet. Settings says "Connecting Meta is coming soon."
- **"That campaign was not found."** Someone else deleted it. Refresh the page.
- **"You do not have permission to make changes here."** Viewers cannot change campaigns.
