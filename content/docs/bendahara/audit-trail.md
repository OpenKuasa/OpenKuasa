The Audit Trail logs every change to the books: who did it, what they did, to which document, and when. The audit trail starts on 23 Oct 2026. Earlier changes were not recorded.

## Who can do this

Anyone in the workspace can open the audit trail. It only shows the log: there is nothing to change on this screen.

## Steps

1. Open **Audit Trail** under **Compliance** in the Bendahara menu.
2. The top cards show **Events today**, **This week**, **Voids this month** and **Deletions this month**. The charts cover the last days of activity across all documents and people.
3. In **Activity log**, narrow the list:
   - **Document type**, such as Invoice, Supplier bill, Payment, Expense, Journal or SST return;
   - **Person**;
   - **Period**: **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days** or **All time**;
   - a document number in the search box.
4. Click **Apply**.
5. Each row shows **When**, **Who**, **What**, **Document** and **Details**. Click **Older** at the bottom to see earlier entries.

If nothing shows, the table says so: "No events match these filters.", or that nothing was recorded in the period.
