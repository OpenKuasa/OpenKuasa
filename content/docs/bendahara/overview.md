The Bendahara Overview is the first screen of finance. At the top you can ask Bendahara, the finance assistant, about your books in plain words. Below it are cards with your figures.

## Who can do this

Everyone in the workspace can open the Overview and ask Bendahara. What Bendahara can change for you is still limited by your role. In the demo workspace the cards show fixed sample figures.

## Before you start

The assistant uses your workspace's AI key, or the free questions each person gets without one. See [Add your OpenRouter key](/docs/getting-started/openrouter-key).

## Steps

### Ask Bendahara

1. Open **Overview** under **Overview** in the Bendahara menu.
2. Type your question in the chat card, or pick a ready one such as **Overdue invoices**, **Who owes us the most?**, **Draft an invoice** or **Unpaid supplier bills**.
3. Bendahara looks up the real figures. When you ask it to change something, such as drafting an invoice or recording a payment, it shows a card for you to approve or reject. Nothing is saved until you approve.

### Read the cards

- **Cash balance**, **Revenue · MTD**, **Outstanding · AR** and **Payable · AP**.
- **Cash in vs out** by week, **Revenue by category**, **Collections**, **Outstanding by customer** and **Collection rate**.
- **Overdue invoices** to chase and **Upcoming bills** to pay.
- **e-Invois · LHDN MyInvois**: the status you recorded on your invoices.
- **Finance AI agents**: links to [AI Agents](/docs/bendahara/ai-agents).

A card with no real source yet shows "Not available yet" instead of sample numbers.
