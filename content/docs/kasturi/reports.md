Reports shows your lead figures for the last 7, 30 or 90 days. It counts the leads in Jebat's lead funnel, together with the contacts that came in through your lead forms, landing pages and chatbot.

## Who can do this

Everyone in the workspace can open Reports and download the leads file.

## Steps

1. Open **Kasturi** and choose **Reports**.
2. Pick the period: **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days** or **Last 90 days**.
3. Read the figures at the top: **Total leads**, **Win rate**, **Show rate** and **Qualified**.
4. Look at the charts:
   - **Leads vs qualified** over the period.
   - **Leads by source**: where leads came from: WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok or Website.
   - **Lead → won**: how many reached each lead stage, from Leads to Contacted, Qualified, Booked and Won.
   - **Qualification rate**: qualified out of all leads.
   - **Top sources**: leads and qualified leads by source.
5. To download the leads for the period, click **Export**. The CSV file has each lead's id, name, channel, stage, source and the date it came in.

## Common problems

- **Leads by state** shows **Not available yet**. It is not built yet.
- The stages here are lead stages, not your deal pipeline stages. Deal figures are on the Deals and Overview screens.
- **That range is not valid.**: pick one of the three periods and try again.
