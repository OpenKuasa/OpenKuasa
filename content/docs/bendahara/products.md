Products & Services is your list of items. When you pick an item on a document line, it fills the line for you: at its selling price on a quotation or invoice, and at its cost on a supplier bill.

## Who can do this

Owners, admins and members can add, edit, archive and delete items. Viewers can only look. In the demo workspace nothing can be changed.

## Steps

### Add an item

1. Open **Products** under **Records** in the Bendahara menu.
2. Click **Add Item**.
3. Type the **Name** and choose the **Type**: **Product** or **Service**.
4. Add a **SKU** and a **Category** if you use them.
5. Enter the **Selling price (RM)**. You can also enter the **Cost (RM)**.
6. Enter the **SST rate (%)**. It starts at 0.
7. Check the **Tax type**: **Sales tax**, **Service tax** or **No SST**. Until you choose one yourself, it follows the rate: 5 or 10 gives **Sales tax**, 6 or 8 gives **Service tax**, and 0 gives **No SST**. For any other rate, choose it yourself.
8. For **Sales tax**, you can add a **Tariff code**. For **Service tax**, you can add a **Service type code**.
9. Set the **Unit**, for example "unit" or "ream".
10. Click **Add item**.

### Change, archive or delete an item

1. Find the item in **All items**. Search by name, SKU or category, or use the **Show** list.
2. Open the row's menu and choose **Edit**, **Archive** (or **Restore**), or **Delete**.
3. For **Delete**, confirm with **Delete item**. This cannot be undone.

## Common problems

- **"Enter a price of 0 or more."**, **"Enter a cost of 0 or more."** or **"Enter an SST rate between 0 and 100."** Check the number you typed.
- **"Another product already uses that SKU."** Each SKU can be used once.
- **"This item is used on bills or other documents. Archive it instead."** An item on any document cannot be deleted.
