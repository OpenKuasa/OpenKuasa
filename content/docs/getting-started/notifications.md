Notifications tell you when something needs you, such as a request waiting for your approval. They appear under the bell at the top of the app.

## Who can do this

Every signed-in person chooses their own notifications, whatever their role. In the demo workspace the choices are read-only.

## Steps

### Read your notifications

1. Click the bell at the top of the app. A number shows how many are unread.
2. Click a notification to open what it is about. It is marked as read.
3. Click **Mark all read** to clear the count.
4. Click **Notification settings** to change what you get.

### Choose what you get

1. Open [Notifications](/account/notifications). You can also find it in the account side menu under **Account**.
2. Under **Delivery channels**, turn **In-app** on or off. With **In-app** off, nothing new reaches your bell, except security alerts.
3. Under **What you get notified about**, tick or untick the **In-app** box for each event:
   - **New leads**
   - **Deal stage changes**
   - **Payments & invoices**
   - **Team activity**
   - **Requests from your team**
   - **Decisions on your requests**
   - **Roster changes**
   - **Shift swaps**
   - **Hiring**
   - **Weekly summary**
   - **Product updates**
4. Click **Save changes**.

You see **Notification preferences saved.**

![The list of events and channels on the Notifications page](/docs-media/getting-started/notifications.png)

## What works today

- **In-app** notifications are live and follow your choices.
- **Email** and **Push** are not connected yet. You can tick them, and the page saves your choice for later, but nothing is sent by email or push.
- **Quiet hours** (**Pause notifications overnight**, **From**, **To**) are saved with your other choices. Times are shown in the timezone from your profile.
- **Security alerts are always on and can't be turned off.** These include a changed password and two-step sign-in being turned on or off.

## Common problems

- **Please choose valid quiet hours.** Pick a time from each list.
- **The demo workspace is read-only. Sign up to save changes.** Demo choices cannot be changed.
