Each interview in **All interviews** has a menu. What it offers depends on the interview's status and on whether its application is still active.

## Who can do this

Owners, admins and members. Viewers see the interviews, but no menu.

## Steps

1. Open **Lekir** and choose **Interviews**.
2. Find the interview in **All interviews** and click the **…** button in its **Actions** column.
3. For a scheduled interview, choose:
   - **Reschedule or edit** to change the time or any detail. Click **Save changes**. If the new time is still ahead, Lekir shows **Interview updated** with a new invitation and calendar file to send.
   - **Invitation and calendar file** to get the invitation again.
   - **Mark completed** if it took place, or **Mark no-show** if the candidate did not turn up.
   - **Mark completed and add feedback** to do both in one step.
   - **Cancel interview**. Confirm with **Cancel interview**, or click **Keep interview** to leave it. It stays in the list as cancelled.
4. For a completed, cancelled or no-show interview, choose **Mark as scheduled again** to put it back.

Nothing is sent to the candidate when you change or cancel an interview. Tell them yourself.

## Common problems

- **Needs an outcome.** The interview's time has passed but it is still marked scheduled. Mark it completed or a no-show.
- **Application rejected** or **Application withdrawn.** The interview is still scheduled, but its application has ended. **Reschedule or edit** is not offered. Cancel the interview if it will not go ahead.
- **No invitation is offered.** An interview whose start has passed has no invitation or calendar file.
- **This interview has feedback. Clear the feedback before marking it as scheduled again.**
- **Nothing was changed: the interview already has these details.**
- **This interview was changed a moment ago. Nothing was saved. Check it and try again.** Someone else changed it. Click **Load the current details** to see it as it is now.
- **This interview is no longer scheduled. Mark it as scheduled again first.**
