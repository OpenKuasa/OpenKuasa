The **Calendar** puts the team's dates on one month view: leave, time-off, public holidays, training, birthdays and work anniversaries. It only shows information.

## Who can do this

Everyone in the workspace. What you see depends on who you are:

- **Owners and admins** see everyone's leave, holidays and milestones.
- **Managers** see their team's dates and the company's.
- **Everyone else** sees their own dates and the company's, and only the training they are enrolled in.

## Steps

![The Lekiu Calendar month view with leave, time-off and training](/docs-media/lekiu/calendar.png)

1. Open **Lekiu**, then **Calendar** under **General**.
2. Read **Away today**, **Away in the next 7 days**, the public holidays in the month and **Next 30 days**.
3. Use the arrows to move a month back or forward. Click **Today** to come back to this month.
4. Each kind of date has its own colour. A pending request is drawn with a dashed outline. Click an entry to open it.
5. **Upcoming** lists what is coming in the next 30 days.
