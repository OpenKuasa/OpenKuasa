**My Documents** collects your documents, the letters issued to you and your paid payslips in one list. Anything waiting for your acknowledgement comes first.

## Who can do this

Any member of the workspace whose account is linked to their employee record. You only see your own documents. Uploading is not available in the demo workspace.

## Before you start

Your account must be linked to your employee record. For an upload, have a PDF or an image (JPEG, PNG, WebP or HEIC) up to 10 MB.

## Steps

### Acknowledge a document or letter

1. Open **Lekiu**, then **My Documents** under **General**.
2. Find a row with the live dot. The **Needs your acknowledgement** count shows how many there are.
3. Read it, then click **I have read this**. This records that you have read it. It is not a signature.

### Upload your own document

1. Click **Upload**.
2. Fill in the **Title** and choose the **Type**: **Identity**, **Medical**, **Certificate** or **Other**. Add **Expires (optional)** if it has an expiry date.
3. Click **Choose file**, pick the file, then click **Add document**.

You can remove a document you uploaded yourself: click **Remove** on its row, then **Remove** again. Documents HR added can only be removed by HR.

### Open a file

- Click **Download** to open a document's file.
- For a letter, click **Open in Letters**. For a payslip, click **Open in My Payslips**.

## Common problems

- **"You can add identity, medical, certificate or other documents. An owner or admin adds the rest."** Contracts, tax forms and benefits documents are added by HR.
- **"That file is larger than 10 MB."** Use a smaller file.
- **"Your browser blocked the new tab. Allow pop-ups for this site and try again."** Allow pop-ups, then click **Download** again.
