When HR issues a letter to you, it appears on **HR Letters** under **Your letters**, and on **My Documents**. Letters waiting for you are listed first.

## Who can do this

Any member of the workspace whose account is linked to their employee record. You only see issued letters addressed to you. Drafts are never shown to you.

## Steps

1. Open **Lekiu**, then **Letters** under **Approvals**.
2. On the letter's row, click **View**.
3. If the letter asks for it, click **I have read this**. This records that you have read it. It is not a signature.
4. Click **Print** to print it, or **Close**.

A letter waiting for you shows **Awaiting acknowledgement**.
