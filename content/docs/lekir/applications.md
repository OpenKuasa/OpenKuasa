The Applications screen lists your applications, newest first. Each row opens a details panel with what the candidate sent and the actions for that application.

## Who can do this

- **Open the details panel, read the cover letter and click View CV:** everyone in the workspace, viewers included.
- **Change, rate, reject, reopen, edit or delete:** owners, admins and members.

## Steps

1. Open **Lekir** and choose **Applications**.
2. In **Recent applications**, find the candidate and click **Details**. Owners, admins and members see **Details** on every row. Viewers see it only where there is something to open: details the candidate sent, or interviews.
3. Read what they sent: **Cover letter**, **CV file** (click **View CV**), **CV** link, **Portfolio** link and **Expected salary**. Only what they gave is shown.
4. Under **Interviews**, see their interviews and any feedback. Interview feedback is separate from the application's own rating.
5. Change where the application stands:
   - Click **Move to …** to move it forward, or **Back to …** to move it back one stage.
   - Click **Mark withdrawn** or **Reject**, pick a **Reason (optional)**, and confirm.
   - A rejected or withdrawn application shows **Reopen** instead.
6. Rate it with the stars, from 1 to 5. Click **Clear rating** to remove the rating.
7. Click **Schedule interview** or **Record a past interview** to open the interview form.
8. Click **Edit candidate** to change the person's details, or **Add to talent pool** to keep them for future roles.

### Delete an application or a candidate

1. In the details panel, click **Delete application** or **Delete candidate**.
2. Read what will be removed. Lekir counts it for you first.
   - Deleting an application also removes its interviews and its CV file. The candidate stays on file.
   - Deleting a candidate removes them with all their applications, interviews and CV files.
3. Click **Delete application** or **Delete candidate** to confirm. This can't be undone.

## Common problems

- **This file is no longer available.** The CV file has been removed. The demo workspace has no files.
- **Couldn't open the CV. Try again.** The link could not be made. Try again.
- **This application is already at that stage.**
- **An application can move back one stage at a time.**
- **Another candidate already has this email.** Each candidate's email must be different.
- **Showing the newest 50 of …** The list shows the newest 50 applications.
- **Couldn't check what this removes. Close this and try again.** The delete is not offered until the counts load.
- **Interviews are not cancelled.** Rejecting or withdrawing does not cancel scheduled interviews. Cancel them on the Interviews screen and tell the candidate yourself.
