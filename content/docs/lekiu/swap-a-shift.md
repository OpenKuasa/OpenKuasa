You can swap one of your shifts for a colleague's. Your colleague accepts first, then a manager or HR approves it, and the two shifts are exchanged on the roster.

## Who can do this

Anyone whose account is linked to their employee record, outside the demo workspace. Both shifts must be after today.

## Before you start

Your account must be linked to your employee record, and you need a shift on the roster in the next 60 days.

## Steps

### Ask for a swap

1. Open **Lekiu**, then **Shift Calendar** under **Attendance**.
2. Find the **Shift swaps** card, under **Ask for a swap**.
3. In **Your shift**, pick one of your shifts.
4. In **For a colleague's shift**, pick the colleague and shift you want instead.
5. Add a **Note (optional)** if you want, up to 500 characters.
6. Click **Ask for the swap**. Your colleague is asked first, then it goes for approval.

### Answer a swap someone asks of you

When a colleague asks you, the card says so. On the swap's row, click **Accept** or **Decline**.

### Cancel a swap

While the swap is waiting for your colleague, you (the person who asked) can click **Cancel**, then **Confirm**. Once your colleague has accepted and the swap is waiting for approval, either of you can cancel it. This cannot be undone.

## Common problems

- **"None of your shifts in the next 60 days can be swapped right now."** You have no shifts that can be offered.
- **"One of you already works on the other day, so those shifts cannot be swapped."**
- **"One of those shifts is already in a swap that is waiting. Cancel that one first."**
- **"Both shifts must be after today."**
