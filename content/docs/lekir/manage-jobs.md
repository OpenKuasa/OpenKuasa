Each job in **All jobs** has buttons to edit it and change its status. The buttons you see depend on the job's status.

## Who can do this

Owners, admins and members. Viewers see the jobs, but no **Actions** column.

## Steps

1. Open **Lekir** and choose **Jobs**.
2. Find the job in **All jobs**.
3. To change its details, click **Edit**, change what you need and click **Save**. You will see **Changes saved.**
4. To change its status, click the button on its row:
   - A **Draft** job shows **Open**.
   - An **Open** job shows **Pause** and **Close**.
   - A **Paused** job shows **Reopen** and **Close**.
   - A **Closed** job shows **Reopen**.
5. To delete a job, click **Delete**, then **Delete job** to confirm. This can't be undone.

Only open jobs with a description appear on your public careers page.

## Common problems

- **This job has applications. Close it instead.** A job with applications cannot be deleted. Close it so it stops taking applications.
- **Add a description before opening this job.** Edit the job and add a description first.
- **An open or paused job needs a description.** You cannot remove the description from a job that is open or paused.
- **The search box and filters do nothing.** **Search jobs…**, **All Departments** and **All Statuses** are not ready yet (Coming soon).
- **Not available yet.** **Avg time-to-fill** and **Filled · YTD** are not built yet.
- **Discard your changes?** You closed the form with unsaved changes. Click **Keep editing** to go back to it.
