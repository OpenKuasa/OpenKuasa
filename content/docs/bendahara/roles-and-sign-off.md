Every finance screen is open to everyone in your workspace. What each person can change depends on their role. The database checks the same rules again, so they hold however a change is made, the assistant included.

## Who can do this

Your role is set by an owner or admin on [Team](/account/team), under Organisation in your account. The roles in finance are:

- **Owner** and **admin**: can do everything, including the steps that need a sign-off below.
- **Member**: can create and change drafts, record and schedule things, and send them for approval, but cannot sign off.
- **Viewer**: can look at every screen and export lists, but cannot change anything.

The demo workspace is read-only for everyone.

## What needs an owner or admin

These steps are an owner's or admin's only. A member who tries one is told so, usually with "Only an owner or admin can post, pay or void. Ask one of them to do this."

- Posting a supplier bill, and voiding a bill or a payment.
- On the sales side: posting and voiding invoices, issuing and voiding credit notes, recording and voiding payments in, and posting and voiding cash receipts.
- Paying now, or marking a scheduled payment, refund or voucher as paid.
- Issuing and voiding supplier credit notes, and applying their credit to a bill.
- Approving, rejecting or sending back expenses, approving or rejecting payment vouchers and purchase orders, and closing a purchase order.
- Recording and voiding transfers between accounts, and setting an opening balance.
- Adding or changing expense and income categories.
- The chart of accounts, manual journals, contra entries and FX revaluations.
- Accounting settings (the financial year, the books lock and document numbering) and SST settings.

When an owner or admin saves a new expense, or a new payment voucher for an expense category, it is approved at once.

## What a member does

A member does the day-to-day work and leaves the sign-off to an owner or admin:

- On a form with **Save and post**, a member's document is saved as a draft, and they are told only an owner or admin can post it.
- A member's expense waits as **Pending approval**.
- A member's payment to a supplier is saved as **Schedule for later**, not paid.
- A member can submit a purchase order for approval. An admin cannot approve an order they raised or submitted themselves; an owner can.
