Cash Receipts lists every payment in: money against customers' invoices and other income. It is part of the cash book. A receipt can wait as a draft. It gets its receipt number when it is posted.

## Who can do this

- Owners, admins and members can create, edit and delete draft receipts.
- Only owners and admins can post or void a receipt. Only they see **Post receipt**, **Post** and **Void**. A member sees: "You can save this as a draft. An owner or admin posts it: until then it has no number and counts as nothing received."
- Viewers can only look. In the demo workspace nothing can be changed.

## Steps

### Record a receipt

1. Open **Cash Receipts** under **Cash Book** in the Bendahara menu.
2. Click **New receipt**.
3. Under **What it is for**, choose **A customer's invoices** or **Other income**.
4. For invoices: choose who it was **Received from**, then tick the invoices under **Invoices paid** and check each amount.
5. For other income: under **Received from**, choose a contact, or choose **Not a contact** and type a **Name** if you want. Choose the **Income category** and enter the **Amount**.
6. Choose the account it was **Paid into**, the **Date received**, the **Method** and any **Reference** and **Notes**.
7. Click **Save draft**. An owner or admin can click **Post receipt** to post it at once.

While a draft receipt for invoices waits, it holds its part of each invoice, so nobody else can receive that part. A draft has no number and counts as nothing received until it is posted.

### Post, edit, delete or void

From the row menu:

- **Edit**, **Post** or **Delete** a draft. Posting gives it its number. After that it can no longer be edited, only voided.
- **Void** a posted receipt. Confirm with **Void receipt**. For invoices, they will owe that amount again; for other income, it no longer counts as money received. It stays in the list as Void and keeps its number. This cannot be undone.

Use the **Period** list to see **This month**, **This quarter**, **This year** or **All time**.

Payments recorded on [Payments in](/docs/bendahara/payments-in) are posted at once and also show here.

## Common problems

- **"That is more than is still owed on an invoice, counting what other receipts not yet posted already hold."**
- **"The invoices on one receipt must all be one customer's, and must still exist."**
- **"A receipt for an invoice in another currency is recorded with Record payment on Invoices."**
- **"A receipt needs either invoices that add up to its amount, or an income category that still exists. Edit it and try again."**
- **"This receipt has been posted or voided, so that can no longer be done."**
- **"This date is in a closed period: the books are locked to …"** See [Accounting settings](/docs/bendahara/settings).
