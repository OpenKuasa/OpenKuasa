**Scorecards** shows performance scores out of 5, taken from reviews. It only shows information. Reviews are written on **Review Scores**.

## Who can do this

Everyone in the workspace can open it.

- **Owners and admins** see the whole team. Draft reviews are listed and marked, but only released reviews count in the averages.
- **Everyone else** sees only their own released reviews.

## Steps

1. Open **Lekiu**, then **Scorecard** under **Performance**.
2. Read the figures at the top.
3. Read **Competencies**: the score for **Delivery**, **Teamwork**, **Ownership** and **Communication**.
4. Owners and admins also see **Scores by department**.
5. Read **Employee scorecards**: each review's period, score and competencies, highest first.
