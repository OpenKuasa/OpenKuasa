Tuah leads a team. For each part of your question, it asks the specialist for that product, then gives you one answer.

## Who can do this

Anyone who can ask Tuah. The team is on by default.

The demo gives sample answers, so no specialist cards appear there.

## Steps

### See who is working

The specialists are:

- **Jebat** for marketing: ads, spend, leads, lead forms, creatives and appointments;
- **Kasturi** for the CRM: contacts, deals, pipelines, follow-ups and the calendar;
- **Lekir** for hiring: jobs, candidates, applications, interviews and the talent pool;
- **Lekiu** for HR: staff, leave, time off, claims, overtime, attendance, payroll and performance;
- **Bendahara** for finance: invoices, quotations, bills, payments, customers, suppliers, the books and reports.

When Tuah asks one, a card with the specialist's name shows in the answer. It says what the specialist is doing, such as **Getting the task…**, **Thinking…** or **Preparing a change…**. When it finishes, it says **Done** and how many steps it took. Click the card to see each step.

A question that covers more than one product goes to each specialist it concerns.

A specialist only prepares changes. Each one still comes to you on a card to approve or reject.

### Turn the team off or on

There is no on-screen setting for this. You switch it in the address bar:

1. To turn the team off, open `/command?team=0`. Tuah then answers by itself, with every product's tools in one place.
2. To turn it back on, open `/command?team=1`.

The choice is kept in this browser until you change it.

## Common problems

- **A card says "Could not finish".** That specialist ran into a problem. Ask again.
- **Tuah will not read a CV.** Only Lekir, working as a specialist, reads a candidate's CV. Turn the team back on with `/command?team=1`.
