**Settings** holds your workspace's HR defaults. You can change the working week here. The leave and overtime defaults are shown, but cannot be changed yet.

## Who can do this

Owners and admins. The screen is hidden from everyone else. In the demo workspace the form is switched off.

## Steps

![The Lekiu Settings screen with working days, leave entitlements and overtime rates](/docs-media/lekiu/settings.png)

_Shown from the sample workspace, where editing is switched off._

1. Open **Lekiu**, then **Settings** under **Configuration**.
2. The **Workspace** card shows your company and team. Use **Edit company** or **Manage team** to change them in your account.
3. In **Working days and attendance**:
   - Tick the **Working days**.
   - Set the **Start time**.
   - Set the **Grace period (minutes)**, from 0 to 240.
4. Click **Save**. The new values apply from the next clock-in.

**Leave entitlements** shows the default days for new balances: annual, medical and emergency leave. **Overtime rates** shows the pay multiplier for a weekday, a rest day and a public holiday. Changing these is coming soon.

To change one employee's leave entitlement for this year, edit the employee. See [Add and edit employees](/docs/lekiu/add-and-edit-employees).

## Common problems

- **"Choose at least one working day."**
- **"Enter the grace period as whole minutes, from 0 to 240."**
- **"This page is for owners and admins of the workspace."** You are not an owner or admin.
