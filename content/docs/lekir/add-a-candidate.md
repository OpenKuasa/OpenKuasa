You can add a candidate yourself, for example someone who was referred to you. Put them forward for a job, or keep them in the talent pool for later.

## Who can do this

Owners, admins and members. Viewers do not see the **Add Candidate** button.

## Steps

1. Open **Lekir** and choose **Candidates**.
2. Click **Add Candidate**. The **Add a candidate** form opens.
3. Fill in **Full name** (required). You can also add **Email**, **Phone**, **Headline**, **Location**, **Skills** (separate them with commas) and **Source**.
4. Choose a **Job**, or leave it at **No job yet**.
   - With no job, the person goes into the talent pool as Available. They do not appear on the board until you add them to a job.
   - With a job, the form shows **For this application**. Here you can add a **Cover note**, a **CV link** and a **CV file** (PDF, up to 5 MB).
5. Click **Save**.

If someone with the same email is already on file, Lekir does not add them twice. With a job, it adds the application to the person you already have.

## Common problems

- **Enter the candidate's name.** The name is required.
- **Enter an email address like name@example.com.**
- **Without an email, this person can't be matched if they apply online later.** This is advice, not an error. You can still save.
- **Pick a job to attach a CV.** A CV file can only be added with a job.
- **Choose a PDF file.** Only PDF files can be uploaded.
- **This file is … Choose one under 5 MB.**
- **Enter a link that starts with https://** The CV link must be a web address.
- **List at most 20 skills.** Or **Keep each skill to 40 characters or fewer.**
- **Only an open or paused job can take a new application.** Only open and paused jobs are offered in **Job**.
- **… has already applied for … Nothing was changed.** This person already has an application for that job.
- **CV files cannot be uploaded right now. Add the candidate without the file, or try again later.** Nothing was saved. Save again without the file, or try later.
- **The CV could not be attached.** The candidate and application were saved, but not the file.
