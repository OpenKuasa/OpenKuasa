The **Employees** screen is your staff directory. Owners and admins add and change employees here. Everyone else can read the directory.

## Who can do this

- **Owners and admins** can add, edit, deactivate, reactivate and delete employees, and set **Reports to**.
- **Everyone else** in the workspace sees the directory only: name, number, department, designation, reports to, type, join date and status. Private details such as salary and NRIC are never shown in the directory.
- People who have others reporting to them also get a **Team** filter with **My team**.

Nothing can be changed in the demo workspace.

## Steps

![The top of the Employees screen with headcount, department and tenure charts, and the Departments card](/docs-media/lekiu/employees.png)

### Add an employee

1. Open **Lekiu**, then **Employees** under **Management**.
2. In **All employees**, click **Add employee**.
3. Fill in the details. Only **Name** is required.
   - **Employee no.**: leave it blank and the next free number is given.
   - **Work email**, **Designation**, **Department**, **Employment type** (**Full-time**, **Part-time**, **Contract** or **Intern**) and **Join date**.
   - **Reports to**: search by name, then pick the person, or **Nobody**.
4. Fill in the **Private details** you have. These are seen only by owners, admins and the employee themselves: **Monthly base salary (RM)**, **NRIC / passport no.**, **Date of birth**, **Phone**, **Home address**, **Bank**, **Bank account no.**, **KWSP (EPF) no.**, **PERKESO (SOCSO) no.**, **LHDN tax no.**, **Emergency contact** and **Emergency contact phone**.
5. Click **Add employee**.

### Edit an employee

1. On the employee's row, click **Edit**.
2. Change what you need. When editing you can also change the **Status**, and the **Leave entitlements** for this year: annual, medical and emergency leave, in whole or half days. Changing one updates this year's balance.
3. Click **Save changes**.

### Find someone

Use **Search name, email, employee no…**, and the **Department** and **Status** filters above the table.

### Deactivate or delete

- Click **Deactivate** to make someone inactive. Click **Reactivate** to bring them back.
- Click **Delete**, then **Delete for good**. This deletes their leave, claims, draft payslips, documents, files and letters too, and cannot be undone. Someone with a paid payslip cannot be deleted: make them inactive instead.

## Common problems

- **"Another employee already has that employee number."** or **"Another employee already has that work email."** Each must be unique.
- **"That would make a loop: … already reports to this person, directly or through others."** Pick a different manager in **Reports to**.
- **"The employee was saved, but "Reports to" was not: …"** The details were saved. Choose **Edit** on their row to set **Reports to** again.
- **"This employee has paid payslips. Make them inactive instead."** Use **Deactivate**.
