Settings holds Kasturi's own rule for deals, your own Kasturi notifications, and a copy of your contacts. It also links to where the rest of your workspace is set.

## Who can do this

- Everyone in the workspace can open Settings.
- Only owners and admins can change the deal rule and export the contacts.
- Everyone can choose their own notifications, except in the demo workspace.

## Steps

### Require a reason when a deal is lost

1. Open **Kasturi** and choose **Settings**.
2. Under **Deal rules**, tick **Require a reason when a deal is marked lost**.
3. Click **Save**.

It applies to everyone, and to Kasturi AI. Deals already lost are not affected.

### Choose your notifications

1. Under **My notifications**, tick what Kasturi should tell you about:
   - **New leads**: when someone sends one of your lead forms, or asks for a person in a landing page's chat.
   - **Deals won**: when someone else in the workspace wins a deal.
2. Click **Save**.

These are yours alone and appear under the bell at the top of the app. Nothing is emailed. The same switches are in **Account, Notifications**.

### Export your contacts

1. Under **Your data**, click **Export contacts (CSV)**.
2. The file downloads. It has each contact's name, email, phone, company, country, status, source, tags and the day they were added.

One file holds up to 50,000 contacts.

### The rest of your workspace

- **Workspace** shows your company and team, and links to where they are set in your account.
- **Pipelines** lists your pipelines. They are changed on the Deals screen.
- **Connections** shows your AI key, email sending, the chatbot on landing pages and automations, the same as Plugins.

## Common problems

- **Only an owner or admin can change this.**: the deal rule is for owners and admins.
- **Only an owner or admin can export the contacts.**: ask an owner or admin.
- If you have switched all in-app notifications off in **Account, Notifications**, your Kasturi notifications will not reach you until you switch them back on.
- **The workspace has more contacts than one file holds. This file has only the first 50,000.**
- Chatbot conversations are deleted 90 days after their last message. Nothing else is deleted automatically.
