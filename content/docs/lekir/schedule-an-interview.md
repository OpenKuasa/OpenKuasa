Lekir books the interview and writes an invitation for you. It does not send anything to the candidate: you copy the invitation into your own email or message.

## Who can do this

Owners, admins and members. Viewers can see the Interviews screen but not the buttons.

## Before you start

To schedule an interview, the application must be active. A rejected or withdrawn application has to be reopened first. Recording a past interview works for any application.

## Steps

1. Open **Lekir** and choose **Interviews**, then click **Schedule interview**. You can also start from **Schedule interview** in an application's details panel or on a card's menu on the Candidates board.
2. If you started from the Interviews screen, type a name or a job in **Find an application**, then choose it under **Application**.
3. Choose the **Date** and **Time**. Both are in Kuala Lumpur time (GMT+8).
4. Choose the **Duration** and the **Type**: **Video call**, **In person** or **Phone call**.
5. Fill in the **Meeting link**, **Place** or **Phone number or details**, depending on the type. This is optional and is shown in the invitation.
6. Fill in **Interviewer** if you want the candidate to see a name. Add **Notes** for your team. Notes never go in the invitation.
7. Click **Schedule interview**.
8. In **Interview scheduled**, click **Copy invitation** and paste it into your own email or message to the candidate.
9. Click **Download calendar file (.ics)** and attach it to your message, so the candidate gets a reminder in their own calendar.
10. If the application is still at Applied or Screening, you can click **Move to Interview**. Click **Done** when you are finished.

![The Interviews screen](/docs-media/lekir/interviews.png)

### Record a past interview

For an interview that already took place, click **Record a past interview** instead. Choose the application, the day and time it started, and under **What happened** pick **Completed** or **No-show**. Click **Record interview**. Nothing is sent and no invitation is made.

## Common problems

- **That time has already passed in Kuala Lumpur.** Choose a later time, or use **Record a past interview**.
- **Choose a date within the next 12 months.**
- **That time has not passed yet in Kuala Lumpur.** To plan an interview, use **Schedule interview**.
- **Choose a date within the last 12 months.** For a past interview.
- **This application already has an interview at that time.**
- **This application is no longer active. Reopen it before scheduling or changing an interview.**
- **Choose a length between 15 minutes and 8 hours.**
- **Keep this under 300 characters.** For the place or link. **Keep the interviewer's name under 120 characters.** **Keep the notes under 2,000 characters.**
- **Couldn't copy. Select the text above and copy it yourself.**
