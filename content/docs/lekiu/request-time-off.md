Time-off is for a few hours off on one day, such as a clinic visit. For whole days, apply for leave instead.

## Who can do this

Any member of the workspace whose account is linked to their employee record, whatever their role. You can only ask for your own time-off.

## Before you start

Your account must be linked to your employee record.

## Steps

1. Open **Lekiu**, then **Time-Off** under **Applications**.
2. Click **Request Time-Off**.
3. Choose the **Day**, then the **From** and **To** times. The end time must be after the start time.
4. Add a **Reason (optional)** if you want, up to 500 characters.
5. Click **Request time-off**. The request appears under **My requests** as **Pending**.

### Cancel a request

1. Find the request under **My requests**.
2. Click **Cancel**, then **Confirm**. Cancelling cannot be undone.

You can cancel while the request is pending, or after it is approved as long as the day has not come yet.

## Common problems

- **"The end time must be after the start time."** Check the times.
- **"Use times like 09:00."** Type the time in hours and minutes.
