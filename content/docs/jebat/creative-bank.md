The Creative Bank is a list of your ad creatives: images, videos and pieces of copy. For each one you note its channel, its status and, if you know it, its click-through rate (CTR).

## Who can do this

Owners, admins and members can add, edit and delete creatives. Viewers can see them but not change them. The demo workspace is read-only.

## Steps

### Add a creative

1. Open **Jebat** and choose **Creative Bank** in the side menu.
2. In the **Creative library** card, click **Add creative**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Name** (required, up to 120 characters).
   - **Type**: **Image**, **Video** or **Copy**.
   - **Channel**: **WhatsApp**, **Facebook**, **Instagram** or **TikTok**.
   - **Status**: **Draft**, **Active** or **Archived**.
   - **Copy / notes (optional)**: the ad text or your notes, up to 2,000 characters.
   - **CTR % (optional)**: from 0 to 100.
4. Click **Save**.

A new creative is not linked to any campaign. Each card shows **Unlinked** or **Linked**.

### Find, edit or delete a creative

- Use the tabs **All**, **Images**, **Videos** and **Copy** to narrow the library.
- To change a creative, click **Edit**, change it, and click **Save**.
- To remove it, click **Delete**, then **Confirm?**. Click **Cancel** to keep it.

### Read the figures

- **Total creatives** counts the whole bank.
- **Avg CTR** and **Avg CTR by format** use only the creatives that have a CTR.
- **Best format** is the type you have the most of.
- **Top performing creatives** lists your five highest CTRs.

## Common problems

- **"Upload" and "Generate with AI" do nothing.** Uploading files and making creatives with AI are not built yet. Add creatives with **Add creative**.
- **"Generated this month" and "Creatives produced" say "Not available yet".** Only the demo shows sample figures in them.
- **"Avg CTR" says "Not available yet".** None of your creatives has a CTR yet. Add one with **Edit**.
- **"That creative was not found."** Someone else deleted it. Refresh the page.
