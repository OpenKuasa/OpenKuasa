The Lead Funnel lists your leads, newest first, and shows how far each one has got: **Lead**, **Contacted**, **Qualified**, **Booked** or **Won**.

## Who can do this

Owners, admins and members can add, edit, move and delete leads. Viewers can see the leads but not change them. The demo workspace is read-only.

## Where leads come from

- Leads you add here yourself, or that you ask Jebat to add for you.
- People who come in through a lead form, a landing page or the chatbot. They are kept as Kasturi contacts and show here with the channel **Website** and an **Open in Kasturi** button. Their stage follows their status in Kasturi, so you change it there.

## Steps

### Add a lead

1. Open **Jebat** and choose **Lead Funnel** in the side menu.
2. In the **Leads** card, click **New Lead**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Name** (required, up to 120 characters).
   - **Channel**: **WhatsApp**, **Facebook**, **Instagram** or **TikTok**.
   - **Stage**: where the lead is now. It starts at **Lead**.
   - **Source**: optional, for example the campaign or event it came from, up to 120 characters.
4. Click **Save**.

![The Lead Funnel screen, with the stage funnel, leads by channel and the newest leads](/docs-media/jebat/lead-funnel-leads.png)

### Move a lead to another stage

In the lead's row, pick the new stage from the **Stage** list. It saves straight away.

### Edit or delete a lead

- Click **Edit**, change the details, and click **Save**.
- Click **Delete**, then **Confirm?**. Click **Cancel** to keep the lead.

### Read the figures

- **Total leads** counts every lead.
- **Qualified** and **Won** count the leads that have reached that stage or gone further.
- **Conversion** is the share of all leads that reached **Won**.
- **Lead funnel** shows how many leads reached each stage.
- **Leads by channel** shows where your leads came from.

## Common problems

- **A lead has no Stage list, only "Open in Kasturi".** It came in through a lead form, a landing page or the chatbot, and lives in Kasturi. Click **Open in Kasturi** and change its status there.
- **"That lead was not found."** Someone else deleted it. Refresh the page.
- **"You do not have permission to make changes here."** Viewers cannot change leads.
